The Founder

The most relentless voice in the room.

Relentless Aaron

Born DeWitt Gilmore in 1965 and raised in Mount Vernon, New York, Relentless Aaron turned a six-year prison sentence into a purpose. Behind bars he wrote dozens of novels from street-level truth. On release he self-published and sold over 200,000 books by hand before signing a landmark 14-book deal with St. Martin's Press.

He went on to write for billionaire rapper 50 Cent under the G-Unit Books imprint at Simon & Schuster, published standouts like Push, The Last Kingpin, and Extra Marital Affairs, launched The Superstar Magazine, and built a multimedia empire through WebFilmBooks. Today he blends storytelling with AI, design, and mentorship.
