## 01 · Monday · Be Nice, Ep. 3: The Extra Umbrella

> Be Nice. Episode three. The extra umbrella.
>
> Mr. Daniels carried two umbrellas every day. People laughed.
>
> Then a storm hit the bus stop, and a young mother stood there with her baby and nothing over her head. He handed her the second one and kept walking.
>
> Twenty years later. Rain on his daughter's first day of college. A stranger held an umbrella over her.
>
> Same blue handle.
>
> What you carry for strangers finds its way home.
>
> Be nice. See you tomorrow.
