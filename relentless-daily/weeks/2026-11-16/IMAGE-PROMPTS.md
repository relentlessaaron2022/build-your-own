# Image prompts: week of Nov 16–22
Style reference: illustrated urban drama. Cel-shaded characters over near-photoreal backgrounds, people reacting to each other.

Generate each image **vertical, 9:16**. Six per episode, in story order. Name them `day16-1.jpg` … `day16-6.jpg`, `ep7-1.jpg` … and upload them here. Each image gets a slight push-in and lands on its line of the voiceover. Any episode without images is built in the Vox style with free footage of real people instead.

**Style line (paste at the end of every prompt):**
`adult animated drama illustration, cel-shaded characters with clean bold linework and realistic proportions, expressive faces and body language, detailed near-photoreal painted background, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16, no text, no watermark`

**Every shot shows people with each other.** Copy each character description word for word every time they appear, so they stay the same person.
