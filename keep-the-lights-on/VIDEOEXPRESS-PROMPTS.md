# Keep the Lights On — VideoExpress prompt sheet

Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920). Episode 1: 30 s = three 10-second clips.
Look: the "Driving Miss Daisy" style from Lemo-Opuscar's OPUSCAR 98 (a Rockwell-style mid-century oil illustration), moved from afternoon sun to Atlanta at night.

## How to work it

1. **Make the reference stills first:** **the bedroom**, **the bathroom doorway** and **her** (from behind, face never fully clear). Every clip starts from one of them or from the previous clip's last frame, so the room and the woman stay the same.
2. **One clear action per 10-second clip,** with slow camera movement. When a prompt has two beats and the second doesn't happen, re-roll it or trim in the editor.
3. **Never ask the AI for text or app screens.** The payment notifications, amounts and the tip message are text you add in the editor. Keep them generic: no real app logo or name.
4. **Faces stay half-hidden:** turned away, in shadow, behind steam, cropped at the mouth or jaw. Write that into every prompt; it's both the story and how you avoid the uncanny AI face.
5. **Keep the intimacy implied,** an embrace, a mouth at the neck, hands at the waist, so the generator's content filters don't block the clip. The suggestion is stronger anyway.

## Master style (paste into every prompt)

> Mid-century magazine-cover oil illustration in the manner of Norman Rockwell, near-realistic figures, faces and hands modelled from a few soft value planes rather than outlines, faint visible brush strokes that follow form, light canvas texture, warm varnish, soft vignette. Night interior in a modest Atlanta apartment: warm amber key light from a phone screen or a bathroom bulb, cool blue-grey shadows, streetlight cutting through window blinds in thin stripes. Muted palette: cream, brick red, bottle green, warm skin, deep blue night. Telling objects in the room. Faces never fully clear: turned away, shadowed or hidden by steam. Vertical 9:16 frame. No text, no logos, no readable screens.

**Avoid:** text, letters, logos, app interface, cartoon faces, flat digital shading, heavy texture filter, photorealism, nudity, empty background.

## Reference stills

**Bedroom:**
> [master style] A small bedroom at night: an unmade bed with two pillows, a phone glowing face-down on the sheet, window blinds striped by orange streetlight, the city of Atlanta dim beyond. Through an open door at the side, steam drifts from a lit bathroom.

**Her:**
> [master style] A Black woman in her thirties in a worn oversized T-shirt, seen from behind and in profile shadow, sitting on the edge of the bed, hair loosely up, the phone's glow on her cheek and hands.

**Bathroom doorway:**
> [master style] A small bathroom seen from the doorway, thick steam, a warm bulb, a frosted shower door with a man's silhouette behind it, water running.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10 | her still | Close over her shoulder: she sits on the bed in the dark holding the phone, its glow lighting her fingers and the line of her jaw. Her face stays turned away. | shower running from the next room; three soft pings at 2 s, 5 s, 8 s |
| 2 | 10–20 | clip 1 last frame | She locks the phone, slides it under the pillow, stands and walks toward the steaming bathroom doorway, unhurried, as if nothing happened. The camera stays low by the bed, holding on the pillow with a faint glow seeping from under it as she leaves frame. | the shower louder; the bathroom door creaks |
| 3 | 20–30 | bathroom still | Inside the steam: a man's arms pull her in under the warm water, his mouth at her neck, her hand pressed to the wet tile. Steam hides both faces. Slow push in. In the last second the camera is back in the dark bedroom on the pillow: one small blink of light from under it, then black. | water, breath, then silence on the cut; one final ping right on the blink |

**Cut on the blink.** End the video on the frame where the light goes out, with no fade.

### Added in the editor (text overlays, generic UI)

- **1 s:** a small notification card fades in over the glow: **+$1,200.00**. It flashes and vanishes in about 1.5 s.
- **4 s:** **+$400.00**, same treatment.
- **7.5 s:** **+$25.00 · tip · "don't stop talking"**, held a little longer (about 2 s).
- Make the cards by hand: a white rounded rectangle, a green dollar amount, a small grey timestamp. No logo, no app name.
- **No subtitles, no music.** Room sound only: the shower, the pings, breath, the city outside.

### Post caption

Only the amount and a timestamp: **$1,625 · 1:47 AM**

## Voice notes (Episode 2)

Record the woman's lines yourself or with a voice actor. AI voices tend to sound flat in a whisper. Keep them suggestive and short; you want the change in her voice heard more than the words.
