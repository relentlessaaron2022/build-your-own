# Keep the Lights On — 1930s Rubber Hose version (VideoExpress)

Vertical 9:16. Episode 1: 30 s = three 10-second clips.
Look: Lemo-Opuscar's "1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon" style. The tone becomes a pre-Code cartoon: cheeky on top, guilt underneath.
The other version of this sheet (Rockwell-style oil, "Driving Miss Daisy") is in `VIDEOEXPRESS-PROMPTS.md`.

## How to work it

1. **Make the reference stills first:** **the phone character**, **her** (from behind, in her bonnet) and **the bedroom**. Every clip starts from one of them or from the previous clip's last frame.
2. **One clear action per 10-second clip.** When a prompt has two beats and the second doesn't happen, re-roll it or trim in the editor.
3. **No text from the AI.** Money amounts and the tip are title cards you add in the editor (below).
4. **Original characters only.** Avoid anything that looks like a known cartoon star; no bobbed flapper with a giant head (too close to Betty Boop), no round black ears, no gloved mouse, cat or dog.
5. **Keep the intimacy implied:** silhouettes behind a shower curtain, steam, a kiss at the neck. 1930s cartoons hinted, they never showed, and the generator's content filters agree.

## Master style (paste into every prompt)

> 1930s black-and-white rubber hose cartoon, hand-inked. Characters have noodle limbs of even width with no elbows or knees, white four-finger gloves, pie eyes (black oval pupils with a wedge cut out), big round shoes, bodies that squash and stretch like balloons, a thick ink outline that wobbles slightly. Flat white, grey and black characters over soft grey watercolour backgrounds. Objects are alive with faces and bounce in time to jazz. Strictly greyscale, rich blacks and bright whites. Old 35 mm film look: flicker, scratches, dust, vignette, rounded film-gate corners. Vertical frame. Original characters only. No colour, no text.

**Avoid:** colour, text, letters, logos, 3D render, modern anime, realistic people, existing cartoon characters, nudity.

## Reference stills

**The phone (the star):**
> [master style] A smartphone drawn as a 1930s rubber hose cartoon character: a slim rounded rectangle with pie eyes and a sly grin on its screen, noodle arms with white gloves, a tiny notification light on its top like a hat feather. It lies on a rumpled bed sheet, grinning.

**Her:**
> [master style] A rubber hose cartoon woman seen from behind, sitting on the edge of a bed in an oversized nightshirt and a puffy night bonnet, noodle arms, white gloves, a phone glowing in her hands. Face not shown.

**The bedroom:**
> [master style] A small cartoon bedroom at night: a bed with two plump pillows that breathe, window blinds with streetlight stripes, a city of tall cartoon buildings outside, a bathroom door ajar with puffy cartoon steam clouds curling out.

## Episode 1 — "$1,600" (30 s)

| # | Time | Start from | Prompt (after the master style) | Sound / music |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10 | phone still | Close on the phone on the bed. Three times it pops its pie eyes open and its screen flashes bright, bouncing a little higher each time; her gloved hands reach in and pick it up. Steam clouds curl in from the bathroom door at the edge of frame. | hot jazz trio, sly and slow; a muted-trumpet "wah" on each flash at 2, 5, 8 s |
| 2 | 10–20 | her still | She locks the phone (its eyes snap shut), slips it under the pillow, then tiptoes toward the steaming bathroom with exaggerated innocent steps, one step per beat. The pillow puffs up, a faint glow leaking from underneath. | tiptoe on a plucked bass, each step a note; the shower hissing |
| 3 | 20–30 | bedroom still | The bathroom door: behind the shower curtain, two silhouettes, his long noodle arms pull her in; steam clouds billow out and cover everything. Then the camera is back on the pillow: the phone peeks one pie eye out, the light blinks once, and an iris closes on it to black. | the band stops dead for the embrace (water and steam only); one tiny xylophone "ping" on the blink, then the iris snaps shut |

**Cut on the blink.** The iris should close on the blink. If the AI won't do the iris, end the clip on the blink and add an iris-out wipe in the editor.

### Title cards (add in the editor)

Period cards: a small near-black plate with a white double-line border, old serif lettering (e.g. IM Fell English SC). Each pops on and off with no fade.

- **1 s:** `+ $1,200.00`
- **4 s:** `+ $400.00`
- **7.5 s:** `TIP: "don't stop talking"` (hold about 2 s)
