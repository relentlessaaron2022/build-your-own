## How to shoot or generate each outfit photo

Each photo is used three ways: full screen at the opening, a face ring in the corner, and the top half of the vertical. These settings let one photo do all three.

1. **Horizontal 16:9, 1920×1080 or larger** (the same shape as the black studio photo). One photo covers both cuts: I crop the vertical from it.
2. **Same seat, same studio, same pose** as the black photo: in the chair, hand at the chin, the mic in front. Change only the outfit. Then the series looks like one show.
3. **Her face in the left half of the frame**, about where it is now, with clear space on the right for the Git Hub Report sign and the logo.
4. **Head and shoulders clear**: nothing crossing her face (the mic sits below the chin, as now).
5. **The same headphones and lighting.** The purple-and-gold studio light ties all three outfits together.
6. **Outfit ideas:**
   - **Red:** deep crimson leather or satin, gold zipper and trim. Same family as the Relentless University maroon.
   - **Gold:** metallic gold jacket with black accents, gold headphones. Keep the background unchanged so the gold pops.
7. **Name the files** `avatar-red.jpg` and `avatar-gold.jpg` (any image format is fine) and send them here.

When a photo arrives, I mark where her face is and where the vertical crop goes (`assets/host-red.json`, `assets/host-gold.json`), render one test frame of each layout, and send it to you before the episodes are built.
