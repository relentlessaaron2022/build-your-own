Can I buy it today?

Yes. The Kindle ebook of Ricky Cool: The Beat on the Block is on sale now on Amazon for $5.99. Buy the ebook.

When is the paperback coming out?

The printed copy is coming next week. Check this site and the Amazon listing then. We do not have a paperback price or an exact hour yet, so we will not invent one. The ebook is available now if you do not want to wait.

What age is it for?

Amazon lists the reading age as 4–8 years. Ricky Cool, the lead, is a 10-year-old. That is a comfortable “look up” age for a read-aloud with a younger child and for an early reader who wants a short comic. See the reading guide.

Who is the author?

Amazon lists Roslyn B. Kraus as the author and Edwin Bayron as the editor.

Is it a comic?

Yes. It is a warm, funny, full-color comic for young readers, and it is Book 1 in the Ricky Cool series. A “Make a Beat at Home” page at the end invites families to clap, tap, and listen together.

Which devices can read the ebook?

This title is a Kindle Print Replica book, which keeps the comic layout fixed, like a printed page. Amazon lists Fire tablets, Kindle apps for Android, iPhone, iPad, PC, Mac, and the web, plus Alexa Tablets and Amazon Kids Tablets. Amazon also says the title is only available on select devices. If you use a basic E-ink Kindle, check Amazon's supported-device list before you buy. More detail is on the Kindle devices page.

Why is the file 91.1 MB?

It is a full-color comic in Print Replica format, so the file is large. Give it a little time to download over a normal home connection.

Does it have page flip, Word Wise, or enhanced typesetting?

Amazon lists Page Flip, Word Wise, and enhanced typesetting as not enabled. That is typical for a fixed-layout comic. Screen reader support is listed as supported.

Can I gift the ebook?

Yes. Amazon lets you send a Kindle book as a gift. The person you send it to can open it in a Kindle app or on a compatible tablet. If you would rather wrap a physical copy, wait for the paperback next week.

Is this the first book in a series?

Yes. Amazon's description says The Beat on the Block is Book 1 in the Ricky Cool series. This site is about Book 1, which is the book you can buy today.
