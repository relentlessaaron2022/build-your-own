What your child will find

On Ricky's block, a bucket goes BOOM. A bike bell goes RING. Claps on the stoop go CLAP. Ricky listens close, writes the sounds down, and invites his neighbors in. Soon the girl on the steps, the man drumming on a bucket, and the kid ringing his bike bell are making music that belongs to everybody.

The lesson at the heart of the book is simple enough for a four-year-old to feel and strong enough for an eight-year-old to talk about: everything has a beat, and everybody has a part to play. When the first try comes out too loud, Ricky figures out that the song works when people take turns.

A warm, funny, full-color comic for young readers who love music, city life, and kids who make something beautiful out of ordinary days. A “Make a Beat at Home” page at the end invites families to clap, tap, and listen together.

Book 1, The Beat on the Block

This is the first book in the Ricky Cool series. It is a short, full-color comic — the print edition is prepared as 34 pages — so a child can get to the last page without losing the thread. After the story, families can make their own beat at home with a pot, a clap, and a spoon on a cup.
