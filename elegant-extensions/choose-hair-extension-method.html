Which hair extensions are best for you?

There's no best method, only the best method for your hair, habits and budget. This is how Waatani narrows it down in a consultation.

The five things that decide it

  1. Your hair's density and strength. Fine hair needs attachments that spread the weight and stay hidden. Thick hair can carry more hair per attachment.
  2. Texture. The added hair has to match your curl, wave or straightness, and how you style it day to day.
  3. The goal. Length, volume, filling thin spots, or covering a hairline call for different methods.
  4. Lifestyle. Swimming, daily workouts, high ponytails and heat styling all change what will last.
  5. Maintenance you'll actually do. Every method needs follow-up. Pick one whose schedule fits your life.

What happens in the consultation

  • A look at your hair and scalp, in natural light.
  • Color matching, sometimes blending more than one shade.
  • A recommendation and the reasons for it, including what he would not do on your hair.
  • A quote and a maintenance plan, so there are no surprises.
