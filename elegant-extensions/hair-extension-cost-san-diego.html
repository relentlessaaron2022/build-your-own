Pricing

What hair extensions cost, and what a quote includes

Extension pricing varies more than almost any salon service, because no two heads need the same hair. Elegant Extensions gives a real quote after seeing your hair. Here's what goes into it.

The six things that set the price

  1. Method. Strand-by-strand bonds take longer to install than tapes or beaded rows. Specialty systems carry different costs.
  2. Amount of hair. A partial fill for volume is very different from a full head.
  3. Length. Longer hair costs more, and needs more of it to look full at the ends.
  4. Hair brand and quality. Premium, ethically sourced human hair from brands like Great Lengths and Hairdreams costs more and lasts longer.
  5. Color work. Blending several shades, or toning, adds time.
  6. Removal of old extensions. Taking out someone else's install, especially a problem one, is its own service.

What to ask so you can compare quotes

  • Is the hair included, and which brand?
  • How many grams, strands or wefts are in the quote?
  • What do move-ups or maintenance cost, and how often?
  • Is removal included at the end?
  • Can the hair be reused at the next install?

Why cheap can cost more

Low-cost installs often save money on hair quality or on time spent placing attachments. That's how slipping, matting and breakage happen, and repairs cost more than doing it right. Compare total cost over a year, including maintenance, rather than the first appointment alone.
