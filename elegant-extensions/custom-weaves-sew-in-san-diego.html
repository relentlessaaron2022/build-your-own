What a sew-in weave is

A traditional sew-in weave starts with your natural hair braided into a flat foundation, often cornrows, along the shape the finished style needs. Wefts are then sewn onto that braided base with a needle and thread. Depending on the look, some of your own hair may be left out to blend over the top, or the install may be fully closed with a closure or frontal.

Custom vs off-the-shelf

A custom weave means the braid pattern, the amount of hair, the leave-out and the cut are designed for your face shape and how you part and wear your hair, rather than installing the same pattern on everyone. Color-matching the wefts to your own hair is what makes a leave-out style read as natural.

Weave or beaded rows?

A GLAMLOCKS beaded sew-in also uses sewn wefts, but anchors them to a row of beads instead of a braided base. Beaded rows can sit flatter and be moved up without re-braiding; a braided-base weave suits textures and protective styles where a braided foundation is preferred. If you're not sure, ask about both.

Protecting your own hair

  • Braids that are too tight cause tension at the hairline. Tell your stylist if anything hurts.
  • Keep the scalp clean and the base dry after washing to avoid buildup and odor.
  • Don't leave an install in past the time your stylist recommends.
