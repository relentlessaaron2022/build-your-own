Hair extension repair and redo

Slipping tapes, matted bonds, visible beads, uneven blending? Waatani's site says that with 10 certifications and nearly 15 techniques, he can redo or repair nearly every type of extension on the market.

Common problems he sees

  • Slipping or lifting tapes: usually from product near the root, oily scalp or old adhesive.
  • Matting at the bonds or beads: often from going too long between maintenance, or sleeping on wet hair.
  • Visible attachments: placement too close to the part or hairline, or the wrong method for fine hair.
  • Poor color or texture match: extensions that don't blend with your own hair.
  • Tension and breakage: attachments too heavy or tight for the hair holding them.

Repair or start over?

Sometimes the fix is a careful removal, a scalp and hair check, and a fresh install in a better-suited method. Sometimes the hair can be reused, re-tipped or re-taped. Because he works in many systems, Waatani can identify what you're wearing and remove it with the right method instead of guessing.

What to bring

  • The name of the system and hair brand if you know it.
  • When it was installed and the last maintenance.
  • Photos of your hair before the install, if you have them.
