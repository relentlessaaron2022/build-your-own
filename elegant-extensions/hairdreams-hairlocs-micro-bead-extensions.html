Hairdreams

Hairdreams is an Austrian extension and hair-thickening brand. Waatani is certified and an educator in it, and the GlamLocks biography credits him as a national platform artist for Hairdreams. He lists three Hairdreams services:

  • Micro Strands: very small strands for fine hair and natural-looking density.
  • Micro Line: an integration system often used where hair is thinning on top.
  • Quikkies: a quicker, removable application.

Which one fits depends on whether you want length, density, or coverage for thinning. That's a consultation question, not a menu pick.

Hairlocs micro beads

Hairlocs is a bead system that attaches strands with small locks rather than glue or heat. Bead methods appeal to clients who want to avoid adhesives and want adjustments that don't require re-bonding.

NXS Tress and shrink tubes

Waatani also lists NXS Tress and shrink-tube applications. Shrink tubes tighten around a strand and a small section of your hair. They're one more option for clients whose hair or lifestyle doesn't suit tapes or bonds.

Strand and bead methods vs wefts

Strand methods let a stylist place hair exactly where it's needed, which is useful for hairlines, partings and patchy thinning. Wefts (tapes, beaded rows) add a lot of hair quickly along a line. Many clients get the best result from one or the other. Some combine them. How Waatani decides.
