One specialist, many methods

Most salons are built around one extension system and steer every client toward it. Waatani's approach is the opposite. His studio site lists certifications across Great Lengths (Ultrasonic cold fusion, Thermal, GL Tapes and GL Apps), Hairdreams (where he is also an educator: Micro Strands, Micro Line and Quikkies), Hot Heads tape-ins, Hairlocs micro beads, NXS Tress, shrink tubes, and GLAMLOCKS beaded sew-in rows, which he co-created. Having that range means the method can follow your hair, your schedule and your budget, instead of the other way around.

What Elegant Extensions does

  • GLAMLOCKS beaded sew-in extensions: hand-tied and beaded rows with a lockstitch, designed to be light, secure and comfortable.
  • Great Lengths: keratin bonds applied by ultrasonic or thermal tools, plus the GL Tapes and GL Apps systems.
  • Tape-in extensions: Great Lengths and Hot Heads tape systems for fast, flat, reusable wefts.
  • Hairdreams, Hairlocs and micro-bead methods: fine-strand and bead systems for clients who want no heat or no glue.
  • Custom weaves and sew-ins: braided-base sew-in work as listed on his service menu.
  • Repair and redo: fixing or re-installing extensions done elsewhere, across nearly every system on the market.

Who comes to Waatani

His own materials describe clients from San Diego television newsrooms and the entertainment business, and work on runway shows, magazines and TV, including extensions for contestants on America's Next Top Model. His LinkedIn profile dates the business to April 1995. But the everyday client is simply someone who wants more length or fullness that doesn't look added, and who wants it to last without wrecking her own hair.
