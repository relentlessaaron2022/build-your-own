What a beaded sew-in is

A beaded-row (or "beaded weft") install anchors a row of small beads close to the scalp along a straight line of your natural hair, then sews a hand-tied weft to that row. There is no glue, no heat bond and no braided cornrow base. GLAMLOCKS is Waatani's refined version of this approach: he describes it as advanced beaded extensions with a lockstitch, designed to be effortless, light-weight and secure.

Who it tends to suit

  • Clients who want a lot of added volume with relatively few attachment points.
  • People who want to avoid adhesives and heat on their own hair.
  • Fine or medium hair that needs the weight distributed evenly across a row.
  • Anyone who wants hair that can be moved up and reused, rather than replaced each time.

Whether it suits your hair depends on density, texture, scalp sensitivity and how you wear your hair up. That's what the consultation decides.

From GLAMLOCKS Sewn In to GL7

GLAMLOCKS began when Waatani enhanced a technique shared by stylist Autumn and named the result after her studio. The pair later developed GlamLocks GL7, described by the company as a patented 7-in-1 system with seven interchangeable methods using a single hair type. More on that story.
