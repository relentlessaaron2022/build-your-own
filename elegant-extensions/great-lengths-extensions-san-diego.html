The four Great Lengths options he offers

Ultrasonic (cold fusion)

Ultrasonic application softens the keratin bond with ultrasonic vibration rather than direct heat. His studio site calls it the highest-quality bonding method, offering the most options to custom-tailor your result: individual strands can be placed exactly where you need density, around the hairline, or in a partial "fill."

Thermal

Thermal bonding uses a heat tool on the same style of keratin strands. Waatani's description: a clean and precise application with undetectable attachments that are brushable, durable and non-damaging.

GL Tapes

Great Lengths' tape system. He describes it as a discreet application that provides more hair for voluminous, luxurious results. See the tape-in page for how tapes compare with Hot Heads.

GL Apps

A double-sided, single-tab tape-in system. In his words, it gives the most undetectable attachment of any tape system on the market, which is why it's often a fit for fine hair and thin areas.

Why use a certified installer

Great Lengths trains and certifies stylists on its tools and bonds. The hair, the bond size and how tightly strands are placed all affect comfort and how your own hair holds up. A certified stylist who also works in other systems can tell you honestly when Great Lengths is the right call and when another method would serve you better.

Questions to bring to the consultation

  • How many strands or tabs does my density need, and why?
  • Bonds or tapes for my lifestyle (swimming, gym, wearing it up)?
  • What does maintenance look like, and how often?
  • How is removal done, and what does it cost to come back?
