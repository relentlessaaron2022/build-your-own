Why this exists

You know what the tones sound like at 2 a.m.

You know the kitchen table that stays set a little longer. The boots by the door. The prayer said quiet so the kids do not hear the worry in it.

Ossie built this for that life — the pride in it, the cost of it, and the people who pay that cost with you. This page and The Fireline newsletter are where that community gathers: to be seen, to be supported, and to make sure no one who gave everything is ever treated like they are gone.

The Last Alarm

They answered their last call. We answer for them now.

In the fire service, the Last Alarm — the final bell — marks a firefighter who will not return to quarters. Here, that bell is not an ending. It is a promise: your name stays spoken, your family stays held, your story stays told.

Use this space to light a candle for someone you carry. Add a name in the newsletter and we will honor them in the next issue’s Remembrance Roll.
