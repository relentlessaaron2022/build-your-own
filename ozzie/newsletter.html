The Last Alarm — what it means

For generations, the firehouse bell called firefighters to duty. When a firefighter died in the line of duty, the bell was rung in a special pattern — a solemn signal that the call was complete and a brother or sister had returned home for the last time.

Each issue of this newsletter ends the same way: with names, dates of service where families provide them, and a moment of quiet before we close.
