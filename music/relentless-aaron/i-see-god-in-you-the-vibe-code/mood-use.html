The title itself tells you the mood this track is aiming at. I See God in You is not a party record and it is not a film-score cue. It is the opening statement of Relentless Aaron’s first album, a 3-minute-42-second R&B/Soul cut featuring Feng Shui Soul, released August 16, 2025.

Where it belongs in a day: early morning, a Sunday reset, a drive when you do not want anything shouting at you, or the stretch of a playlist reserved for prayer, journaling, and catching your breath. The album that follows this song keeps returning to soul language — therapy, stillness, breath, rewind, rise again — so the title track is doing the work of setting that tone before the longer cuts arrive.

It is a short opening door. At 3:42 it will not fill an hour alone, but it is the right length to open a soul or faith-rooted playlist and then hand off to the rest of I See God In You (The Vibe Code), including the lead single “Hit Rewind” at track 4 and the closing album cut “While We’re Alive.”
