People search for a title in more than one way

Authors and musicians get searched in more than one way. Someone types the title. Someone types a character, a track, or a theme. Someone asks whether the book is right for their kid, or where to listen to the song. One homepage cannot carry all of that well.

This package builds five microsite pages around one title, release, or offer you already have. Each page takes a different search angle and has its own copy. None of the five is a rewrite of another. That is the house standard: three to five distinct pages per title, not a long set of thin, repeated pages.

For a book, the five angles might be

  • The title page
  • The characters
  • The theme or lesson
  • A page for parents or educators
  • A practical “who it’s for” page

For a song or album, they might be

  • The track itself
  • The album it lives on
  • The theme of the lyric
  • A page for fans who already know the artist
  • A practical “where to listen” page

You do not need a 40-page site to be taken seriously. You need a tight set of pages that do not copy each other.
