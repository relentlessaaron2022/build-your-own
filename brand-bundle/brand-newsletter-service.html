When a brand qualifies for sub-genre letters

Some brands should not put every audience in one letter. A children’s line and an adult catalog are not the same reader. Two musical lanes may not be the same listener. A flagship offer and a second business line may not be the same customer.

If brand research shows you have distinct sub-genres or sub-audiences that each deserve their own letter, we add up to three additional newsletter issues — one per qualifying sub-genre.

That extra is not automatic. It is decided during the qualification and brand-research review, written into your scope, and agreed before you pay. If you do not qualify for the extras, you still receive the one branded newsletter.
