The set, title by title

Each card is a title in the planned set, not an invented checkout. The names in these summaries are the names already used in the book files: Zara, Amara, Dre, Jordan, and Kofi. Early brand notes marked most of those book-file names, other than Malik, as placeholders.

Coming soon · Book 1 of 5

Sink or Float?

Zara and Amara put objects in a bowl of water, guess wrong, and learn that testing beats guessing. Project notes mark Book 1 as uploaded to KDP, but no live store listing was found on November 15, 2026.

Coming soon · Book 2 of 5

Tower Power!

Zara and Dre build a paper-tube tower that keeps falling until they make the base wider. Notes mark Book 2 as a full 32-page build and uploaded; no live store listing was found on November 15, 2026.

Upcoming · Book 3 of 5

The Kindness Fix

Dre knocks over a friend’s block house by accident. He and Zara rebuild it together and say sorry. Project notes say the book is about two-thirds built, with pages still missing, so this page labels it Upcoming.

Upcoming · Book 4 of 5

Shadow Giants

A flashlight turns a tiny toy dinosaur into a giant shadow, and Jordan and Zara learn that a shadow is blocked light. KDP status notes list Book 4 as not started. A cover file already exists.

Upcoming · Book 5 of 5

Game Time!

Kofi, Zara, and Amara each want a different game, so they take turns, make a plan, and find a way to do both. KDP status notes list Book 5 as not started. A cover file already exists.

How the books connect to the show

The five-book plan is drawn from show ideas that already have title cards or episode files: a sink-or-float test, a tower that needs a wider base, a kindness repair, a giant shadow that is only blocked light, and a playtime that works when friends take turns. The books are 32-page picture-book builds in the project notes, not board books, and not a claim that every matching episode is already streaming.

Questions

Can I buy these books today?

Not from a verified live store link. Books 1 and 2 are marked Coming soon. Books 3, 4, and 5 are marked Upcoming. The Kindness Fix is only partly built. Shadow Giants and Game Time! have not been started.

Is Sink or Float? the Sesame Street book of the same name?

No. This Sink or Float? is a Blacknificent Kids TV picture book. Other books share the question as a title.
