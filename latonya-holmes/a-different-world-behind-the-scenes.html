Behind the scenes · A Different World

Becoming Dr. Henry

How a Georgia-born theatre actress came to play a role in Netflix’s revival of A Different World, told in her own videos and posts.

How it happened

The text. A message from a friend, Brandi, opened the door, and showrunner Felicia Pride invited her into the room. “At the time, there was no Dr. Henry,” she says. “I was simply grateful for the opportunity to show up and do the work.”

The table. Before she was cast she worked as a table reader, and she kept coming to the reads after she had filmed her scene. “Because it was never about ego. It was about the work.”

The audition. In October 2026 she posted her audition tape next to the finished scene. Her advice to other actors: “Trust your preparation, trust your instincts and KEEP DOING THE WORK!”

The reveal. On premiere day, Sept 24, 2026, she posted a photo of herself beside her trailer sign on Instagram: “Well… now I can finally tell y’all. Meet Dr. Henry.”
