Dr. Henry on A Different World

On Netflix’s A Different World, LaTonya Holmes plays Dr. Henry, the therapist counseling Dwayne Wayne and Whitley Gilbert as their marriage hits its hardest stretch. Her scene comes in Season 1, Episode 3, “Center de Stage.”

The scene

Homecoming weekend brings the Hillman legends back to campus, and the episode cuts to a therapist’s office. Dwayne (Kadeem Hardison) and Whitley (Jasmine Guy) have been separated for almost six months, and Dr. Henry has been seeing them for weeks. She presses them to say plainly what they want: keep working on the marriage, or move toward divorce. When Whitley lists Dwayne’s virtues, Dr. Henry names what she sees between them, which is balance.

“You know, I’ve been seeing you two for weeks now. I know you’ve been separated for almost six months. I know no one’s filed for divorce, but I do not know why.”
— Dr. Henry, “Center de Stage”
