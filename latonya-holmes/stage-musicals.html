Stage & musicals

Trained in musical theatre at Roosevelt University, she built her career on stage before moving into TV.

Highlights

Joseph Jefferson Award

Won in 1995 for Actress in a Supporting Role in a Musical, playing Lorrell in Dreamgirls at the Marriott Theatre, Lincolnshire, Illinois.

The Color Purple

First Broadway national tour, as standby for Celie and Nettie.

Little Shop of Horrors

First Broadway national tour, directed by Jerry Zaks, as Ronnette.
