Stage & musicals

She trained in musical theatre at Roosevelt University and built her career on stage: regional houses, The Muny, Paper Mill Playhouse, a world premiere and two first national tours.

Highlights

Joseph Jefferson Award

She won in 1995 as Actress in a Supporting Role in a Musical, playing Lorrell in Dreamgirls at the Marriott Theatre in Lincolnshire, Illinois. That same year she played Lorrell at Paper Mill Playhouse.

Little Shop of Horrors

She played Ronnette in the first national tour of the Broadway revival, directed by Jerry Zaks, from 2004 to 2006. Before that she played Crystal at The Muny.

The Color Purple

From 2007 to 2010 she toured with the first national company, covering Celie, Nettie and Squeak. She met Quincy Jones on opening night.

Where it started

At Northside High School in Warner Robins she was the starting point guard until someone told drama teacher Gerald Ray Horne that she could sing. He cast her in the one-act The Wiz, and Thespian Troupe 2892 became her home. When The Color Purple tour played Atlanta in 2008, the casting announcement listed her as one of two Warner Robins natives in the company.
