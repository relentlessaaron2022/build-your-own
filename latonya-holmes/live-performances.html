Highlights

Singing for a cause

The 2014 Steve Chase Humanitarian Awards marked 30 years of Desert AIDS Project. Palm Springs Life listed her among the night’s featured singers, alongside Jane Krakowski and Eden Espinosa. She also sang at Divas Simply Singing!, a long-running Los Angeles benefit concert. “If I can speak to one person a week … to know that I’m helping someone pay a bill, buy his or her medicine, then that’s what I’ll keep doing,” she told A&U Magazine.
