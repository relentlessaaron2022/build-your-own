Singles: “Love Hangover” & “Things Change”

Two singles, two stories. One is a fan-funded take on a Motown classic that raised money for AIDS charities. The other is an original about letting go and loving yourself first.

“Love Hangover” (Single Version) · 2013

She launched the project on Indiegogo in spring 2013, and 138 backers took the campaign to success. A follow-up campaign she called “Coffee for a Cause” kept donations open. Her version of the Diana Ross classic came out July 19, 2013. Part of its proceeds went to APLA and other AIDS organizations, and she sang it at the 23rd annual Divas Simply Singing!

  • Apple Music: Love Hangover

“Things Change” · 2016

“Things Change” was produced by Myron McKinley, who has worked with Earth, Wind & Fire and Howard Hewett. It came out in spring 2016, and SoulTracks featured it as a “First Listen.” She wrote it after a long relationship ended.

“‘Things Change’ came to me one night, waking me out of my sleep at 4 in the morning. I went on my balcony and wrote it right then. … You are responsible for your own growth. … Love yourself first.”
— LaTonya Holmes, EURweb (2016)
