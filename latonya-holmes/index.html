LaTonya Holmes

LaTonya Holmes is an actress, singer and voice actor from Warner Robins, Georgia, now based in Atlanta. She trained in theatre at Chicago’s Roosevelt University, won a Joseph Jefferson Award for playing Lorrell in Dreamgirls, toured the country in The Color Purple and Little Shop of Horrors, and has worked across TV, film, animation and R&B recording. In 2026 she joined Netflix’s A Different World as Dr. Henry.
