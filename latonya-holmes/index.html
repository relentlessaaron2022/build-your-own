LaTonya Holmes

LaTonya Holmes is an actress, singer, songwriter and voice actor from Warner Robins, Georgia, and is based in Atlanta. She trained in musical theatre at Roosevelt University in Chicago and won a Joseph Jefferson Award for playing Lorrell in Dreamgirls. She toured North America in the first national tours of Little Shop of Horrors and The Color Purple. Her work since then spans network TV, animation, R&B recording and live concerts. In 2026 she joined Netflix’s A Different World as Dr. Henry.

Career timeline

  1. Warner Robins, GAA starting point guard at Northside High School, she was steered to drama teacher Gerald Ray Horne, who cast her in a one-act production of The Wiz. It was her first show.
  2. ChicagoShe earned a full scholarship to Roosevelt University and graduated summa cum laude with a BA in Theatre. She majored in musical theatre and minored in music.
  3. 1989She played Betsy in a touring production of Big River.
  4. 1995She played Lorrell in Dreamgirls at Paper Mill Playhouse in New Jersey and won the Joseph Jefferson Award for the role at the Marriott Theatre in Lincolnshire, Illinois.
  5. 1996–1999She played Crystal in Little Shop of Horrors at The Muny in St. Louis, then joined Paper Mill’s Children of Eden (1997), where she understudied Stephanie Mills and sang a “Wasteland” solo on the cast album. In 1999 she played Celia Vane in the world premiere of Dorian in Los Angeles.
  6. 2000–2003She sang background vocals on Snoop Dogg’s Tha Last Meal and Shade Sheist’s Informal Introduction, played in the all-girl band in Monkeybone, and began voicing Flo the Kitten on PBS’s Clifford’s Puppy Days.
  7. 2004–2006She toured North America as Ronnette in the first national tour of Little Shop of Horrors, directed by Jerry Zaks.
  8. 2007–2010She joined the first national tour of The Color Purple, covering Celie, Nettie and Squeak. When the tour played Atlanta in 2008, the casting announcement billed her as a Warner Robins native.
  9. 2008She released her debut album, Love Me Just for Me, and opened for Eric Benét at Crash Mansion in Los Angeles.
  10. 2010–2012She appeared in two episodes of Hannah Montana (Disney Channel) and as Ruth in Grey’s Anatomy (“Second Opinion,” Nov. 15, 2012).
  11. 2013–2014Her “Love Hangover” single was funded by 138 Indiegogo backers, and part of its proceeds went to AIDS charities. She sang at Divas Simply Singing! and at the 20th Steve Chase Humanitarian Awards in Palm Springs.
  12. 2015–2016She guest-starred on IFC’s Maron, played the lead in the short Beast Mom, did additional voices for Mafia III, released “Things Change” (produced by Myron McKinley) and played Lorrell again at The Black Rep’s 40th-anniversary Dreamgirls concert.
  13. 2019–2024Her credits include Queen Sugar, Blind Sight, Peace in the Valley and Lifetime’s Tempted by Love: A Terry McMillan Presentation. In 2020 she joined Paper Mill’s Dreamgirls reunion livestream.
  14. 2025She played Foreman Shirley in two Season 4 episodes of BET+’s All the Queen’s Men.
  15. 2026She plays Dr. Henry in Netflix’s A Different World (S1 E3, “Center de Stage,” Sept. 24). Netflix renewed the series for Season 2 on Oct. 2.
