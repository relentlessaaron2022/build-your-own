Love Me Just for Me

Love Me Just for Me is LaTonya Holmes’s seven-track R&B/soul debut, released March 10, 2008. It opens with “Gone On” featuring Phat Cat and closes with the bonus track “Google Me.”

About the album

She wrote six of the seven tracks on her debut, working with producers and co-writers she chose herself. The bonus track “Google Me” was produced by Felton Offard and co-produced and co-written by LaTonya. A&U Magazine names the label as Pecan Tan Music. The CD came out in a limited pressing, with digital release through CD Baby and iTunes.
