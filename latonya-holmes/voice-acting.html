The voice behind Flo

She was the “sassy voice” of Flo the Kitten on PBS’s Clifford’s Puppy Days, a series-regular role. Her animation résumé runs from Warner Bros. and Klasky Csupo to Adult Swim and 2K’s Mafia III. She trained in animation voice technique with Ginnie McSwain in Los Angeles. Her vocal range runs from alto through mezzo-soprano to soprano, and she does a Southern American accent.
