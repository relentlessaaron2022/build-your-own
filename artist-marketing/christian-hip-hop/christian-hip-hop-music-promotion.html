Promotion without the cringe, and without the hard close

Christian hip-hop music promotion fails when it sounds like a tract, and it also fails when it pretends the faith is not there. The writing should sound like the artist: direct, specific, and usable in a caption or on a page.

100 social posts, five pages, 50 articles, and a newsletter give you that language. You publish the posts unless the scope says otherwise.

Invite people to the music, then to the rest

A campaign can announce a record, tell the story of a verse, point to a date you already have, or invite people to a list. It should not promise spiritual results we cannot deliver, and it should not sell the artist as a gimmick.

No fake testimonials. No invented endorsements from churches or famous ministries.
