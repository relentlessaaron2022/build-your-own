Name the band, name the lane, make it findable

Rock is a house with many rooms: classic, alternative, hard, guitar-pop, blues-based, punk-adjacent. Independent artists lose time when the bio says “rock” and nothing else. Marketing for independent rock artists starts by describing the actual sound and the actual unit — a four-piece, a duo, a solo artist with a rotating band.

Bookers and listeners should be able to tell, in a short page, whether you belong in their room. That is not a limitation. That is useful.

The myth of “just play live and it will spread”

Live work still matters. It does not automatically produce a good website, a clear catalog, or posts you can use from the road. The bundle is built to fill that gap: five pages, 100 social posts, 50 articles, and a newsletter.

We will not write a stadium story around a club band. We will write a true story around the band you are.
