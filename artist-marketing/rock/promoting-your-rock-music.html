EPs, albums, and the single that leads

Promoting your rock music means being exact about the format. An EP is not a debut album. A live session is not a studio record. A lead single should point to the project it comes from, with credits for who wrote and who played.

If the band’s character changes from record to record, the pages can say so instead of smoothing everything into one blur.

Keep the back catalog within reach

People who discover you from a new track will look for the earlier work. If that work is unlisted, untitled, or only on a forgotten platform, you lose them. The web pages can hold the path.
