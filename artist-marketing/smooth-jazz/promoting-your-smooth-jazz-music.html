Lead instrument, featured guests, clean titles

Promoting your smooth jazz music means putting the song title, the lead instrument, and any featured player or singer on a page that will still be there next year. People often search that way. A streaming tile alone will not carry the credits.

If a record is built for radio length, say so. If a live version exists beside a studio version, distinguish them.

Catalog for a long listening life

This music is often lived with over years, in cars and offices and weekend rooms. The web pages can hold the catalog in an order that makes sense, with a suggested place to start.

We do not promise adds or chart numbers. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter.
