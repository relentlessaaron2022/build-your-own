A gospel record deserves the same care as the service it came from

Promoting your gospel music is not an afterthought to the recording. Titles, personnel, whether the date is live or in studio, and where to listen or buy all belong on a page that does not vanish after release week.

If a song is already being sung in churches, say so only if it is true. If you want people to start with one track, name it. If there is a choir or a featured voice, credit them.

FAQ

Can you promote a live worship recording?

Yes. The copy should say it is live, name the context you want public, and credit the people on the recording.

Will you sell physical CDs for us?

No. We can point to the place you already sell or stream the music.

What if some songs are hymns in the public domain and some are originals?

Then the writing should say so, with accurate credits on the originals.
