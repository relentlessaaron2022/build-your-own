The artist and the ministry can share a page without confusion

Gospel artists are sometimes told to choose between being a minister and being an artist. In public, the better move is usually clarity: who you are, what you sing, which group or house you stand with if that is part of the story, and what music is available now.

Marketing for independent gospel artists, as we do it, does not invent a celebrity testimony. It writes what is true and useful. It does not mock the church, and it does not use faith as decoration on a generic artist template.
