R&B promotion should feel like the record, not like a street-team script

The genre is intimate. Listeners notice when the caption sounds nothing like the singer. Promotion that works here is consistent, specific, and calm enough to let the voice do the heavy work. It is also organized: a place to send people, a reason to return, and language that can be reused without going stale.
