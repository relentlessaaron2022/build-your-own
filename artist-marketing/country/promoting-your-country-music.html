Let the song have a page, not only a streaming tile

Promoting your country music is about the writing as much as the release date. People still want to know what a song is about, who wrote it, who played on it, and whether there is an album around it. A streaming tile will not carry that load.

If you co-write, say so. If a track came out of a writer round, say so. If the record was cut live or built in a small studio, that belongs in the copy when it is true. Specific is more country than vague swagger.

Singles, albums, and the long life of a story song

A story song can find its audience slowly. Pages and articles that stay up are how a record keeps working after the first weekend. The bundle is built for that kind of life: a home for the catalog, posts you can use at release and later, and a newsletter for people who already care.
