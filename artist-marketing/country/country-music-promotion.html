Country promotion should be usable in a real week

Independent country artists often promote between day jobs, church, family, and a weekend run of rooms. The assets have to be easy to use: posts that can go up without a design department, pages that already explain the date or the record, a newsletter that can go to a list on a Tuesday.

That is what this promotion offer is. It is not a bus tour. It is not a radio buy. It is not a promise that a festival will call.

Talk to listeners like adults

Country audiences are used to being sold to. They are also used to spotting a fake. The posts and articles we write are meant to be specific and calm — about the song, the show, the record — without a carnival of punctuation.
