Give the single a place to live after midnight

Trap soul records often find people at odd hours. The page they land on should still work: a clear title, a way to listen, a short account of the song, and a path to the project around it. Promoting your trap soul music is that kind of housekeeping, done with taste.

If your catalog is a sequence of singles, the writing can treat it as a sequence, not as a failed album. If there is a project title that holds the songs together, the pages should use it.

Do not over-explain a feeling. Do not under-identify a record

The copy should not lecture the listener about heartbreak. It should also not be so vague that two of your songs could share the same paragraph. Titles, features, release dates, and a precise sense of the sound belong on the page.

We do not promise a playlist home. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter around the music.
