A funk artist is often a crew, and the page should show it

Independent funk lives in bands, even when a bandleader’s name is on the poster. Marketing that erases the group in favor of a solo-star script usually feels wrong. The public face should name the unit, the roles, and the kind of funk you play — raw, polished, go-go adjacent, jazz-leaning, or straight dance-floor.

People hire and follow a feeling, but they search for a name. If the name is not attached to a clear page, the feeling stays in the room where it happened.

Live reputation is not the same as a searchable brand

You can pack a local room and still be hard to find from another city. Five web pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter are how a live reputation starts to travel in writing. We will not invent a national story you have not lived.
