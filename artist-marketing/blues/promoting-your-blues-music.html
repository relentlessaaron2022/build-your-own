Treat the date as a document

Promoting your blues music starts with the facts of the recording: live or studio, who played, where, which originals and which traditional songs, how to listen. Those facts are the respect the music deserves.

If you have a new studio record and a pile of live favorites, the pages can help a listener tell them apart. If a song has been in the set for years and is only now recorded, that is worth saying.

Keep older records in the room

Blues catalogs are often long-lived. A new release should not hide the work that already proved the artist. A simple catalog path on the web pages is part of the promotion, not a side note.

We do not promise festival slots or radio. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter around the music.
