Albums still matter in this lane

Neo-soul listeners often want a full body of work, not only a single hook. Promoting your neo-soul music can mean writing around a sequence — why the record opens where it does, who played on it, what the live version changes — so the album is treated as an album.

If you release singles, we will not pretend they are a boxed set. If you have a live session that is the real document, the pages should say that.

Liner-note seriousness, without the dust

Personnel, writing credits, the studio or the room, the year: these details are not trivia here. They are how serious listeners enter the work. The web pages and the articles can carry them without sounding like a press kit from 1998.

We do not promise critic coverage or playlist adds. We build a durable home for the catalog, plus posts and a newsletter.
