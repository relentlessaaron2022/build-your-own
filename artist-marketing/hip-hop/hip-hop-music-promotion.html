Promotion without the fake hustle language

Hip-hop music promotion has a bad reputation in some corners because the market is full of people selling miracles. We do not sell miracles. We sell a defined bundle of pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter, after research.

The posts should sound like you, or like a clean version of how you already talk — not like a stranger writing “go stream this.” The pages should be useful when a stranger lands on them at 1 a.m. The articles should still make sense six months later.

A campaign needs a destination

Every post, every story, every video should have a place to send people. If that place is only a streaming link, you are renting attention from a platform that will not explain you. Five web pages, each on a different angle, give you a destination you control.
