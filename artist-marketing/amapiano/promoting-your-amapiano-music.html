Long mixes, radio edits, and features

Promoting your amapiano music means labeling the work correctly. A seven-minute mix and a short edit are different documents. Vocal features and producer credits belong on the page. If a piano riff is the identity of the record, the writing can say so without turning into a music-theory lecture.

Point from a new single to earlier work, and from a feature back to your own catalog, so the dance leads to the artist.

A catalog that can be browsed, not only danced to
