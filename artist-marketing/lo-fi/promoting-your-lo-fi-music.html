Give the tape a spine

Promoting your lo-fi music means presenting a beat tape, an album, or a single as a dated work with titles, credits, and a place in your catalog. A track can live in a playlist and still have a home on a page that says it is yours.

If the music is meant for studying, working, or late listening, the copy can say that. It should also say what the project is called, so it is not only “lo-fi beats.”

Separate the project from the wallpaper

Background listening is a real use. It should not be the only story. Articles and pages can talk about the writing, the samples you have the right to discuss, the players, or the idea behind a tape.

We do not promise placements on famous 24-hour streams. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter.
