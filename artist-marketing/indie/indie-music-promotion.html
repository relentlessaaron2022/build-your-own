Promotion that does not feel like a sellout script

Indie music promotion can be direct without being loud. Announce the record. Tell the story of a song. Point to a date you already have. Invite people to a list. That is a campaign.

100 social posts, five pages, 50 articles, and a newsletter give you the materials. You publish them unless the scope says otherwise.

Stay specific, stay modest, stay findable

Avoid empty “please stream and share” loops. Name the song. Name the town. Name the player. Articles can go longer on how the record was made without inventing a myth.

No fake press quotes. No bought reviews.
