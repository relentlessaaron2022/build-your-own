Treat the EP like it matters, because it does

Promoting your indie music often means taking a small release seriously: personnel, recording notes, the order of the songs, where to listen or buy, including Bandcamp or a shop if that is part of how you sell the work.

A bedroom record and a studio record can both be presented with dignity if the copy is honest about how they were made.

Keep the back catalog on the map

First EPs disappear when a newer one arrives unless someone keeps the path. The web pages can hold that path, and articles can point people to a place to start.

We do not promise blog coverage or playlist adds. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter.
