Independent is the method. It still needs a public face

Indie can mean a sound, a budget, or a stance. Marketing for independent indie artists, as we do it, does not try to make you look like a major-label campaign. It does try to make you look findable: a name, a true bio, a catalog, a way to hear the work, a way to read about it.

If you are a band, the page should feel like the band. If you are a solo songwriter, the page should not be dressed up as a four-piece. Accuracy is part of the ethic.

DIY does not mean doing the writing twice at midnight

Many indie artists already write their own posts and are tired. The bundle is extra hands for the pages, the articles, a run of posts, and a newsletter — still in a voice you can stand behind, because you review the work.

Five distinct pages keep the story from repeating. Up to three sub-genre newsletters may be added if research finds a true sub-lane.
