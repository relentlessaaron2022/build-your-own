Be exact about the artist and the tradition you stand in

Reggae is not one sound. Roots, rocksteady-leaning work, lovers rock, dub-minded records, and newer session music each ask for different language. Marketing for independent reggae artists starts by telling the truth about which of those rooms you actually stand in, and who you are in that room — singer, player, group, or all three.

We will not write a tourist version of the culture around your name. We will write a clear, useful public face for the artist you are.

Local reputation still needs a page the world can open

A strong name in a city or a session circuit can still be hard to research from elsewhere. Five web pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter give that name a place to live. If you work across diaspora audiences, the writing can say so without pretending you are everywhere.
