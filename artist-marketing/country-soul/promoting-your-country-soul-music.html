Name the instruments and the feeling, then play the record

Promoting your country soul music works best when the writing is concrete: the vocal, the guitar, the pocket, the lyric. People who love this music are looking for a particular blend. If the page is shy about that blend, they will keep moving.

Releases in this lane may be singles that sound like short films, or albums that feel like a Saturday night and a Sunday morning. The copy should tell a new listener which one they have found, and where to go next in the catalog.

Do not hide the soul. Do not hide the country.

Some artists sand off one side of the music to seem more “marketable.” That usually makes the record smaller. Our job is not to sand you down. It is to present the work accurately and make it findable.

We do not promise a format-radio home that may not exist yet. We do promise, in the written scope, a set of pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter built around the music you actually made.
