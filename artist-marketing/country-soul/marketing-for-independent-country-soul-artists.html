If your lane has two names, your page has to hold both

Country soul sits at a crossing: country songcraft and soul vocal, sometimes with gospel grain, sometimes with a rhythm section that leans R&B. Independent artists in this lane are often told to pick a narrower costume. You do not have to. You do have to explain yourself, or the internet will keep filing you in the wrong drawer.

Marketing for independent country soul artists starts with language that is proud and exact. Not “a little bit of everything.” The actual mix. The actual voice. The actual songs.

Playlists will not do this work for you

Format playlists are built for songs that already behave. A country soul record can be homeless between “Hot Country” and “Soul Chill” even when the song is strong. A set of pages you control, written around the artist, is how you stop relying on someone else’s bin.
