Originals, remixes, and mixes are different documents

Promoting your EDM and house music means labeling the work correctly. An original, a remix, a bootleg you are allowed to post, and a recorded DJ mix should not share one vague paragraph. Versions, features, and labels or self-releases belong on the page when they are public facts.

If the mix is the main way people meet you, the writing can treat the mix as a real release, with a date and a length, and then point to the originals behind it.

Keep the catalog tighter than your hard drive

Producers often have more files than public stories. The web pages should present the work you want people to find now, with a path to earlier records that still matter.

We do not promise Beatport chart positions or playlist adds. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter.
