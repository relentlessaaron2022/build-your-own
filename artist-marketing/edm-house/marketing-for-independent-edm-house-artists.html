Decide how the name is used, then write everything to that name

Some artists use one name for DJ dates and another for productions. Some use one name for both. Marketing for independent EDM and house artists starts with that decision, then builds pages that a promoter and a listener can actually use.

The copy should also name the lane with some precision — house, tech house, deep house, club-minded electronic music — so you are not filed under a word you do not play.

A residency and a Discogs page are not a brand home

They are useful. They do not replace a set of pages that explain who you are, what you have released, and how to hear a mix or an original. Five web pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter can hold that center.

We will not invent festival headlines. We will not fake a support-slot history.
