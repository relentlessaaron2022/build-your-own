The single is the door. The catalog is the house

Promoting your pop music should treat the single as a door, not as the entire building. A page for the new song should point to the project, the previous release, and the artist, so a new listener can stay.

If you are in a singles season, say so. If an EP or album is the real statement, the writing should orbit that, not only the lead track.

Titles, credits, and the next listen

Writers, producers, features, and release dates belong on the page. Pop listeners may come for a hook, but the ones who stay often care who made the record. Clear credits are part of looking professional.

We do not promise playlist placement or a chart debut. We build pages, posts, articles, and a newsletter around the music.
