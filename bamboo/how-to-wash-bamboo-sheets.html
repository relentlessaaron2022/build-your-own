Care steps taken from the current sales page

This how to wash bamboo sheets guide is not a generic internet recipe. It follows the care FAQ already published on the Bamboo Solid 2000 Series sales page. If the sewn-in label on the set you receive says something different, follow that label and mention the difference when you call.

StepWhat the sales page says
First washWash once before the first night to clear the finish and let the set settle.
Water and cycleWash cold, at or under 86°F (30°C), on gentle or delicate.
DetergentUse a small dose of mild liquid detergent. Skip powder if it does not dissolve first.
LoadGive the set room in the drum. The sales page says zippers, towels, and a packed load are what pill the face.
Do not useNever chlorine bleach. Never fabric softener. Never dryer sheets. Skip dry cleaning.
DryerDry on low only. Pull the sheets while they are still slightly damp and smooth them on the bed. High heat is described as the move that shrinks them and turns them rough.
Line dryLine dry in shade if you can. Direct sun is said to fade the solid colors.
IronIron only if you must, on low, while the cloth is damp. The sales page says the 2000-series label says no ironing.
How oftenAfter the first wash, every 1 to 2 weeks is listed as enough for most beds.
PillowsSame rules for Cool Comfort bamboo pillows: cold, gentle, low dry, no bleach, no softener. Zip the cover if it has one, and do not crush the fill on high heat.

Unknown: we have not independently re-tested these instructions against a lab or a physical label in hand for this page. Treat the sales page and the label that arrives with the set as the authority.
