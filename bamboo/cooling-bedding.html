Cooling bedding, with only the facts already published

Shoppers looking for cooling bedding usually want sheets that do not sleep hot, and care steps that do not ruin that feel. This page does not invent a cooling technology name or a degree-off-the-skin number. It restates what is already on the Bamboo Solid 2000 Series sales page.

That page says the sheets are cool to the touch and breathe all night. It also warns that fabric softener and dryer sheets “coat the fiber and kill the cool, moisture-moving hand you bought the set for,” and that high heat shrinks the set and turns it rough. If cool sleep is the reason you are buying, those care rules matter as much as the fabric name.

You can pair the 6-piece sheet set with Cool Comfort Bamboo Pillows, Queen ($40 per set) and King ($50 per set). Sheet sets themselves are $20. Shipping is a flat $12 per sheet set. We recommend ordering two or more sets in one box.
