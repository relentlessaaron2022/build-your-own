A cockroach, a giant by any measure, overwhelmed by creatures a fraction of its size. How is that even possible?

The Ant Kingdom is a short documentary about the mystery and the magic of ants: about 20 quadrillion of them alive right now, roughly 2.5 million for every person on Earth. Nobody is in charge. The queen gives no orders. And still, they build armies, farms, living rafts and empires.

Inside:
• The numbers: how scientists counted the world's ants
• Nobody is in charge: pheromones, trails and collective behavior
• Case file 1: the army ants and their living nests
• Case file 2: the first farmers, 50 million years before us
• Case file 3: the fire-ant raft
• Case file 4: the 6,000 km empire
• The lesson: what the little ones teach us about unity

One ant is small. A thousand ants are a force.
