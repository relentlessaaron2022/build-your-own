## Sources for the facts

- Schultheiss et al., "The abundance, biomass, and distribution of ants on Earth," *PNAS* 2022: ~20 quadrillion ants (conservative), ~12 megatons dry carbon, more than wild birds and mammals combined. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9546634
- More than 12,000 known ant species (AP coverage of the PNAS study). https://news.mongabay.com/2022/12/how-many-ants-live-on-earth-at-least-20-quadrillion-scientists-say/
- Army ants, *Eciton burchellii*: colonies of hundreds of thousands of workers, raid fronts up to 10 m wide, living bivouacs. https://www.antwiki.org/wiki/Eciton_burchellii · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eciton_burchellii
- Ant fungus farming began roughly 50 million years ago (estimates range ~50–66 million); leafcutters ~8–12 million years ago. https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/how-ants-became-worlds-best-fungus-farmers-180962871/
- Fire-ant rafts: Mlot, Tovey & Hu, *PNAS* 2011; rafts float for days to weeks. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3093451 · https://www.sciencenews.org/article/teamwork-keeps-fire-ants-high-and-dry
- Argentine ant supercolony ~6,000 km from Italy to the Spanish Atlantic coast; no fighting within, fights to the death with rivals; introduced ~1920 probably by ship: Giraud, Pedersen & Keller, *PNAS* 2002. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC122904/
