# THE ANT KINGDOM, DOSSIER CUT: treatment
Style: **Halftone Dossier** (Lemo-Opuscar). Produced by Relentless Aaron. 16:9 plus a 9:16 vertical, 30 fps. Re-cut of the existing narration (am_onyx, 6 min), no photos.

## Three structures
1. **Case file 006 with exhibits and four case files.** *Chosen.* The script already says "open four case files", so the dossier frame fits.
2. **A wanted poster for the cockroach, flipped:** the giant is the subject and the ants are the evidence. Fun, but it loses the second half.
3. **A ledger:** every number stamped into a running tally. Too dry for the lesson at the end.

**Opening:** the cockroach on dark ink, with ants arriving one by one and then by the dozen (dot density = emotion). OUTNUMBERED.
**Turn:** Exhibit 03, the jaws: ×1 becomes ×1,000, then COLLECTIVE BEHAVIOR, and four folders slide in.
**Ending:** silence, then the UNITY stamp. Four hundred little ant glyphs fly in and form one giant ant ("something much bigger than themselves"). The file goes into a drawer of folders marked COLONY.
