# THE HONEYBEE FILE: treatment
Style: **Halftone Dossier** (Lemo-Opuscar). Produced by Relentless Aaron. 16:9, plus a 9:16 vertical. 30 fps (style rate). Narrated, about 3½ minutes.

## 1. Three structures
1. **The case file:** the honeybee is "charged" with keeping the world fed; five numbered exhibits prove it; a verdict stamp. *Chosen.* It uses the style's apparatus (case number, exhibits, stamps, mugshot) for something grand but gentle, and it lets every verified fact be one exhibit.
2. **A missing-person file, backwards:** a hive goes quiet; we work back through the evidence of what bees do. Strong mystery, but it would push toward colony collapse claims we haven't verified for this film.
3. **The declassified report:** a redacted page lifts one line at a time until "a third of your food" shows. Good hook, but thin for 3½ minutes.

**Opening:** the evidence bag: one honeybee, alone on a dark ink ground, then the file assembles around her (style variation: "the evidence bag").
**Ending:** the verdict. ESSENTIAL slams. Then the file slides into a drawer full of identical folders labelled HIVE, HIVE, HIVE: one bee, one file among thousands (the colony theme, played as scale).
**Score shape:** synth pop-print toolkit, 96 BPM. Sneaky pizzicato and finger snaps under the opening, a marimba-pluck theme per exhibit, stripped to bass and snaps for the heat ball, **silence before the verdict stamp**, music-box bell tag on the drawer.

## 2. Logline and arc
*The honeybee stands accused of keeping the world fed, and the evidence is overwhelming.*
Setup: a tiny suspect, a big charge. Turn: Exhibit 4, where 500 small bodies beat a giant, and the case becomes about the many, not the one. Ending: ESSENTIAL, "because none of them works alone"; her file joins thousands.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Learn from:** police-procedural title sequences and pop-art comic covers: one idea per panel, huge type, a hard graphic rhythm; Saul Bass–era motion for using a single bold shape per beat.
- **Don't take:** any characters, logos, case-file graphics, melodies or shots from them.

## 6. Cue map
96 BPM, 4/4 (one bar = 2.5 s).
- Every exhibit title lands on a downbeat.
- Stamps land on a beat, with a one-bar silence before the verdict.
- Section beds:
  - opening: pizzicato and snaps;
  - exhibits 1–3: kick and marimba theme;
  - exhibit 4: bass and snaps only, dark;
  - exhibit 5: pad and marimba;
  - verdict: silence, then stamp, then bell tag.

## 8. Type and subtitles
- Headlines: black slab serif, misregistration shadow.
- Numbers: fat rounded display.
- Case numbers and HUD: heavy mono.
- Captions: the style's dark-ink pill, with the keyword in honey yellow, carrying the spoken line.
- HUD: CASE 007 and an exhibit chip top-left; a REC dot and the date top-right.
- Palette: manila paper / sepia-black ink / teal overprint / mustard-honey highlight / vermilion stamps.
