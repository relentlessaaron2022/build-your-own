# THE CLIFF FILE: treatment
Style: **Halftone Dossier** (Lemo-Opuscar). Produced by Relentless Aaron. 16:9 plus a 9:16 vertical, 30 fps. Narration Kokoro am_onyx, 3:16. Facts in RESEARCH.md.

## Three structures
1. **Case file 008: the subject lives where you would not stand.** Five exhibits plus a sixth, "the real killer", then the verdict. *Chosen.* It lets the predators be exhibits and saves the honest twist: winter kills more kids than eagles or cats.
2. **Predator lineup:** eagle, cat and winter in a police lineup, with the goat as witness. Fun, but the feet and the dam don't fit.
3. **Altitude log:** a running elevation meter, one fact per 100 m. Strong graphic, but it repeats.

**Opening:** a goat on a ledge the size of a dinner plate, with a dashed drop marked AIR.
**Turn:** Exhibit 03, twelve years of watching and SUCCESSFUL EAGLE ATTACKS: 0. The mother stands over her kid, horns up: DEFENDED.
**Twist:** Exhibit 06 says it is not the eagle and not the cat. WINTER. 40–60% of kids die in their first winter (BC). COLD. HUNGER. SNOW OVER THE FOOD. OUTLAST IT.
**Ending:** silence, then THE CLIMB IS THE DEFENSE. Then STAY UP THERE. HOLD ON. OUTLAST THE WINTER, with the goat on a peak. The file goes into a drawer of folders marked CLIFF.

Score: pizzicato under the open, kick and marimba under the exhibits, bass and snaps under the cat, pad under the winter and the verdict, one bar of silence before the stamp, and a bell tag.
