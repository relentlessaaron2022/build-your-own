## #3 — The 72 rule (Build streams) · 45 sec

**On-screen hook:** Your money has a doubling clock. Most people never check it.

**Script:**
Your money has a doubling clock. Most people never check it.

It's called the rule of 72. Take 72. Divide by your return. That's how many years it takes your money to double.

Eight percent? Nine years. Twelve percent? Six years.

Now flip it. Credit card at 24 percent? Your debt doubles in three years. Same math. Working against you.

This is why I keep saying build streams, not hours. Hours don't double. You run out of them. A stream that earns while you sleep can double while you sleep.

Do the math on your own numbers tonight. Then tell me which clock you're on.

**Caption:**
72 divided by your rate equals the years it takes to double. Works for savings. Works for debt too, and that's the part nobody teaches. Save this and run your own numbers.
