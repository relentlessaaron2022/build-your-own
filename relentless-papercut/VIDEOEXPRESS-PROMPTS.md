# One Sheet — VideoExpress prompt sheet

A 90-second channel trailer for Relentless Aaron, built from short AI clips.
Format: 16:9, 1920×1080. Assemble in VideoExpress's timeline (or any editor).

## How to work it

1. **Make the reference stills first** (text-to-image or a first frame from text-to-video): one of **the man**, one of **the street**. Re-roll until you love them. Save them.
2. **Every shot below starts from a still** (image-to-video), never from text alone. The man has to look like the same paper figure in every shot.
3. **Never ask the AI for words.** Leave out book titles, RELENTLESS and subtitles; add them as text in the editor.
4. **Keep each clip 4–6 seconds.** Use "extend" only when a shot needs more length.
5. Paste the **master style** at the start of every prompt, then the shot prompt.

## Master style (paste into every prompt)

> Handmade cut-paper animation. Every object is a flat piece of paper cut with scissors, laid on black and charcoal paper. China-red paper cut-outs with visible fibre texture, slightly uneven hand-cut edges, a thin double outline cut just inside each shape, small soft shadows under each paper layer. Detail comes only from shapes cut out of the paper: slits, sawtooth rows, small holes. No gradients, no painted shading, no outlines drawn in ink, no 3D, no photorealism. Layers stay flat and parallel to the camera. Characters move like jointed paper puppets, in slightly stepped motion. Night palette: black and charcoal grounds, red figures, warm amber light only where a lamp or window glows. No text, no letters, no logos.

**Negative / avoid** (if the tool has a field for it): text, letters, watermark, realistic skin, 3D render, glossy, gradient, cartoon outline, anime, extra limbs, blurry.

## Reference stills

**The man (character sheet):**
> [master style] A grown Black man in profile, cut from red paper, short cropped hair, neat beard, wearing a simple jacket, standing on black paper. Full body, side view, plain charcoal background, centred.

**The street:**
> [master style] A Harlem block at night in layered cut paper: far skyline in dark charcoal paper, a row of brownstones with zigzag fire escapes in deep red paper, street lamps in front casting amber pools, punched-hole stars. Wide, flat, layered like a paper diorama.

## Text to add in the editor (not in the AI)

- **Shot 7:** PUSH · SUGAR DADDY · THE LAST KINGPIN, one per book as it lands.
- **Shot 14:** RELENTLESS large, centred in the glow, appearing letter by letter. Underneath: RELENTLESS AARON · BOOKS · MUSIC. At about 84 s: SUBSCRIBE.
- **Subtitles:** turn on auto-subtitles, styled as cream text on a dark red strip, low in frame.

## Narration

Record it yourself if you can; your own voice beats any AI voice for this brand. Otherwise use the tool's voiceover with a deep, calm, unhurried male voice. Leave real silence at 41–44 s and 68–71 s.

## Music

A slow late-night jazz bed (upright bass, brushed drums, muted trumpet, a little vibraphone). Drop the music out for the two silences; bring it back hard on the unfold (shot 11) and on RELENTLESS (shot 14). Use only music you have the rights to.
