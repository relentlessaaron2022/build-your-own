# One Sheet — treatment

Style: **Red Paper-cut** (`papercut-red`), picked at random.
Topic: the Relentless brand — Relentless Aaron (street fiction, music) and the circle around it.
Format: 1080×1920 vertical (Reels / TikTok), 24 fps, 60.0 s. English narration (Kokoro, offline, `am_onyx`), burned-in banner subtitles + `.srt`.
Brief defaults (user asked for "random"): no material of their own, no storyboard stop. No logos or photos: every figure is a fictional cut-out stand-in.

## Three structures

1. **A letter.** A folded note unfolds panel by panel; each panel is a chapter of the career. Clear, but it's the style's stock idea and reads like a slideshow at 60 s.
2. **An assembly line.** Books come off a cut-paper press one by one, each cover a pattern. Good for the catalogue, but it turns a person into a product and loses the interior story.
3. **One sheet (chosen).** The whole film is cut from a single red sheet. A lone man writes by a barred window; the pages go to the street hand to hand; then the sheet is folded and cut once, and unfolds into many — books, a microphone, readers, a crowd. It ends with the light off and only the cut word RELENTLESS glowing. The medium's own move (fold – cut – unfold) *is* the turn of the story: one story, cut right, becomes many.

Opening image: scissors biting into a red sheet in the dark — a hook in the first second, no logo.
Ending: the scissors from the opening are set down; the cut letters are backlit and project onto the wall. Bookend: it began with a cut, it ends with what the cut made.
Score shape: starts with nothing but scissor snips as rhythm; builds from a lone zither to a full chamber ensemble at the unfold; drops to a single erhu line and a held breath; one big gong on the last title.

## Logline and arc

A man alone with a pen and a barred window cuts his way out one page at a time, and one story, cut right, opens into a whole world.

- **Setup** (0–17 s): solitude, the work, the pages piling.
- **Push** (17–31 s): the work goes to the street, hand to hand; the titles stack up.
- **Breath** (31–34 s): near silence. One sheet in his hands.
- **Turn** (34–49 s): fold, fold, fold, one cut, unfold — the sheet becomes a rosette of many figures that multiplies across the wall.
- **Ending** (49–60 s): the lights go out except behind one cut piece: RELENTLESS, projected.

## Benchmark

- **Saul Bass's cut-paper title sequences (late 1950s).** Learn: one idea per frame, bold torn/cut silhouettes, the cut as rhythm, type that is part of the image. Don't take: any shape, arm, layout or lettering of theirs.
- **Lotte Reiniger's silhouette films.** Learn: profile acting with jointed limbs, readable at a glance, gestures held long enough to land. Don't take: characters, ornaments, any scene.

## Narration (English, Kokoro `am_onyx`, speed 0.9)

- L1 — Every story starts with a cut.
- L2 — He wrote where most men put the pen down. Behind a wall, by a window with bars.
- L3 — Page after page. No deal. No permission. Just the next line.
- L4 — Then he took the pages to the block. Hand to hand.
- L5 — Push. Sugar Daddy. The Last Kingpin. Every book, another cut in the same sheet.
- L6 — Fold it once. Cut it right. And one story opens into many.
- L7 — Novels. Music. A whole circle, rising with him.
- L8 — Relentless Aaron. Stay relentless.

Facts: L2 (writing while incarcerated) and L4 (selling books hand to hand) are biographical; flagged to the user to confirm before posting. Series names come from the brand brief.

## Subtitles and titles

- Subtitles: a deep red banner strip (`#8e0f14`, darker than the film's reds) with serrated edges and swallow-tail ends, cream Fraunces SemiBold ~46 px, low in frame (y ≈ 1640), dropping in on two 12 fps steps. Never over the subject: subject staging keeps the bottom fifth clear.
- Book titles (bar 7–10): short cut-letter banners that drop onto the trunk — the only place text is part of the action.
- Final title: RELENTLESS in yang-cut letters (counters are real holes), letter by letter on 12 fps steps, backlit; a gold-foil square seal cut by code with "RA" beneath. Held ≥ 6 s.

## Palette

Night film, not festive: ground family = **black / charcoal dyed paper** (far layers lighter charcoal `#2b2a2e`, near darker `#151417`), hero = China red `#d2201f`, near pieces `#de2e25`, far pieces `#a8171a`, antagonist/heavy (bars, car) `#8e0f14`. Gold foil only on the final seal and the backlit glow.
