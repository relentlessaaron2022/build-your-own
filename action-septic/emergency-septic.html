Treat it as urgent when sewage is in the house, drains stay slow after the ground dries, the alarm keeps going off, the ground over the tank has settled, or the drain field stays wet long after the rest of the yard is dry. If sewage reached a basement or crawl space, ventilate, wear gloves, clean, and disinfect; stay away from wet electrical parts on a pump or alarm; and don't dig around a wet tank or field.

After a major storm, the crew may talk through whether the ground is still too wet to work and whether the tank is flooded. Alpharetta residents can also use the Alpharetta emergency page.
