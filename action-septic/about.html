Action Septic Tank Service is located in Alpharetta-Milton, Georgia, in North Fulton County. The company provides residential, commercial, and industrial septic and sewer service for North Atlanta, Metro Atlanta, and surrounding areas, and has done so for more than 20 years.

Matt Banta is the owner and chief operator. The official Company Info page states that he is a fully licensed and state-certified septic contractor in Georgia. In the company's own words, he has spent more than 20 years working under North Georgia yards, and he'll tell you plainly what you have and what, if anything, it needs.

The work

Official services include septic pumping and cleaning, maintenance, inspections, installations, line repair and replacement, drain field repair and replacement, hydro jetting, sewer line cleaning, sewer line repair and replacement, dry wells, alternative systems, backhoe work, backflow prevention, excavating, septic treatments, and grease trap cleaning and care.
