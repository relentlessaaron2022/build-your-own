Facts left off, pending your confirmation

These items showed up in directories or reviews, but not clearly enough on your official site to publish. Nothing on this list has been added to the pages. If you want any of it public, say the word and it goes on. If you want it kept off, it stays off.

  1. Street address. Some directories list one. Your official site does not. It may be a residence. It is omitted.
  2. Business email. An address appears on one third-party listing only. It is omitted until you confirm it.
  3. License or certification number, and the issuing board. The pages say you are a licensed, state-certified Georgia septic contractor, which your Company Info page states. They do not invent a number.
  4. Exact founding year. Your official wording is “20+ years” / “more than 20 years.” That is what the pages use. A founding year from an unverified listing was not used.
  5. Hours. Your official site says 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily. Some directories say 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. The pages follow your official hours. Please confirm if that is still correct.
  6. Google rating and review count. Public aggregators do not agree with each other. No star rating or review count is on the pages.
  7. Naming your son, or calling the company “family-owned.” That appears in some public reviews, not on your official Company Info page. It is omitted until you say you want it used.
  8. A single county list. Your Company Info page and your Service Areas page do not list the same counties. The pages describe service across North Atlanta / Metro Atlanta / North Georgia and list the popular cities, without inventing one official county roster.
