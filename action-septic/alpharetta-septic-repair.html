What a repair visit is for

  • Damaged inlet or outlet baffles
  • Line repair or replacement between the house, the tank, and the field
  • Root intrusion and blocked filters
  • Cracks, deterioration, or signs of leakage once a tank is opened
  • Pumps, alarms, and other components that aren't doing their job
  • Distribution box problems that send too much water to one part of the field

Not every problem means the entire system has failed. A clear look at the tank, the accessible pipes, and the drain field is how you find out whether a repair will do. For Alpharetta jobs that do require a new tank, lines, or field, see replacement and installation.

Questions homeowners ask

Is pumping the same as a repair?

No. Pumping removes sludge and scum from the tank. A repair addresses a damaged part — a line, baffle, filter, pump, or field. Action Septic notes that these are not always the same service.
