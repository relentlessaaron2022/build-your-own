The drain field only works if liquid can soak into the ground and if solids have been kept in the tank. Action Septic Tank Service repairs and replaces drain fields and often points to North Fulton's red clay as the reason local experience matters: clay holds water, so extra flow from a leaky toilet, a sump pump, or a neglected tank has less room to go.

Warning signs they publish include spongy or extra-green grass over the field, standing water or odor after the rest of the yard dries, drains that slow after rain then recover, and a tank that refills unusually fast after pumping. Pumping will not automatically revive a failed field, but skipping pumping is a common way a field gets there.
