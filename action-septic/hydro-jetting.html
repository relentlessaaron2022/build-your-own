Hydro jetting and line cleaning

Hydro jetting for stubborn septic and sewer lines.

Grease, buildup, and roots don't always yield to a simple cable. Action Septic Tank Service offers hydro jetting and line cleaning. Call 770-922-1434.

Hydro jetting is on Action Septic Tank Service's official list, alongside sewer line cleaning and septic line repair. The company has also written about when a line needs jetting rather than snaking — a useful question if you've had the same clog come back.

Jetting uses high-pressure water to cut through grease, sludge, and some root intrusion in septic and sewer lines. It is not a substitute for replacing a collapsed pipe, and it is not a substitute for pumping a tank that's full of solids. It's one tool for a blocked or restricted line, used after someone has a sense of what the line is actually doing.

If drains are slow or you suspect a blockage between the house and the tank, call 770-922-1434 and describe it. Open daily 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., with emergency service available.
