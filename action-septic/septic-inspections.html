Action Septic Tank Service lists inspections among its official services and has recommended annual inspections on older posts, while later guides also cite the EPA's general advice to have a household system inspected at least every three years. The exact visit depends on what you order.

The EPA describes a professional evaluation as potentially including sludge and scum measurements, leakage checks, backup staining, pipe examination, electrical checks, and a look at the drain field. Action Septic's sale inspections follow that same basic list, including the distribution box and the field. In North Fulton clay, the field is often where trouble hides.

If you're buying or selling, a general home inspector may only flush toilets and watch indoor drains. That tells you very little about the yard. Book a separate septic inspection and ask for a written report. Details for North Fulton closings are on the buyer and seller guide and the Alpharetta inspection page.
