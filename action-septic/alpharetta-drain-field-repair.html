The drain field is where treated liquid from the tank is supposed to soak into the soil. In Alpharetta and the rest of North Fulton, that soil is often red clay, which doesn't drink water quickly. When solids escape the tank, when too much clear water pours in from a sump pump or a running toilet, or when a distribution box sends all the flow to one line, the field is the part that suffers.

Action Septic Tank Service lists drain field repair and replacement among its official services, along with excavating, backhoe work, dry wells, and alternative systems. The company writes often about fields that stay mushy days after a rain, grass that's greener in a strip over the lines, odors at the field, and systems that struggle after storms.

Signs the field, not just the tank, is in trouble

  • Soggy, spongy, or extra-green grass over the drain field
  • Standing water or sewage odors that remain after the rest of the yard has dried
  • Drains that slow down after heavy rain, then recover a few days later
  • A tank that fills back up to the outlet unusually fast after pumping
  • Sewage backing up into the house

A wet yard after a storm is not automatically a failed field. Action Septic has also written about waiting until the ground can handle equipment, and about not pumping a flooded tank in some conditions. If the symptoms last after the weather clears, call 770-922-1434 and describe what you're seeing.

Protecting a field still in good shape is usually about pumping on time, keeping grease and non-biodegradables out of the drains, keeping downspouts and sump pumps away from the system, and growing grass — not parking, paving, or building — over the lines. Questions homeowners ask

Do you replace drain fields in Alpharetta?

Yes. Drain field repair and replacement are listed on the company's official services, and Action Septic is based in the Alpharetta-Milton area.

Can pumping fix a failed drain field?

Pumping is maintenance, not a cure for every problem. If the field is already clogged or saturated, the system may need further evaluation and, in some cases, repair or replacement.
