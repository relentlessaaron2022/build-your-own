Why local soil matters here

North Fulton's red clay doesn't drain like sand. A gallon of extra water — from a leaky toilet, a sump pump tied into the plumbing, or a downspout aimed at the field — takes up the same room in the drain field as a gallon from the shower. That's why Action Septic talks so often about clear-water overload, distribution boxes, and pumping before a field is ruined. If something feels off in an Alpharetta yard, especially after rain, call and describe it before the problem moves into the house.
