How often to pump and inspect a septic system.

The right interval depends on the tank and the household. Here's the guidance Action Septic Tank Service publishes, alongside the EPA figures they cite. Call 770-922-1434 to get on a schedule.

There isn't a single calendar date that fits every house. Tank size, the number of people, water use, a garbage disposal, and how full the tank was last time all matter.

What Action Septic has recommended

On official posts about work in Alpharetta, Milton, Dunwoody, and Marietta, the company has recommended scheduling annual septic inspections and pumping a residential tank every two to three years. Commercial tanks, they say, usually require more frequent pumping because of faster flow, more grease, stronger chemicals, and more users.

What the EPA guidance they cite says

In later guides, Action Septic also points to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's general advice: have a household system inspected at least every three years, and pump every three to five years, with the correct schedule depending on tank size, residents, water use, garbage-disposal use, and the amount of solids already in the tank.

How to set your own interval

  • Write down the date of the last pumping and keep the service report.
  • Ask how full the tank was — that tells you whether to come back sooner or later.
  • Have the effluent filter cleaned at each visit if you have one.
  • Call when you notice slow drains or gurgling, not after the yard turns spongy.
  • If the house just got more occupants, or you're hosting often, don't assume the old interval still fits.

Pumping and inspecting are two different things. A freshly pumped tank can still hide a bad baffle or a failing field. Ask what's included, and keep a drawing of the system if you have one. To schedule in Alpharetta or elsewhere in the service area, call 770-922-1434.

Questions homeowners ask

Should I pump every year?

Not automatically. Action Septic has often recommended pumping residential tanks every two to three years, and also cites EPA guidance of every three to five years. Annual inspections are a separate recommendation on some of their posts. Your last report is the best guide.

Do commercial tanks run on the same schedule?

Usually not. The company says commercial tanks typically need more frequent pumping.
