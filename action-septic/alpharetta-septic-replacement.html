Replacement is a bigger job than a pumping or a baffle repair. It can mean a new tank, new lines, a new drain field, or a full installation on a property that needs a system put in correctly from the start. Action Septic Tank Service lists installations, line repair and replacement, drain field repair and replacement, excavating, backhoe work, dry wells, and alternative systems among its services.

The company's own writing draws a clear line between two visits. A pumping is relatively quick: find the lid, remove sludge and scum, look at baffles and the effluent filter, and tell you how the tank looks. A drain field replacement is a different kind of day — excavating, a backhoe in the yard, new lines, and sometimes a soil evaluation and county permitting before work starts. The lawn takes time to come back. That's why they push routine care in Alpharetta and North Fulton: poor maintenance is often what turns a pumping into a field job.

When replacement comes up

  • A tank that is cracked, deteriorated, or leaking
  • Lines that can't be repaired
  • A drain field that no longer absorbs wastewater — soggy ground, standing water, or odor that doesn't clear after dry weather
  • A new installation, or work tied to a larger change on the property
  • Some sites need an alternative system (pumps, aerators, or mounds) rather than a conventional field

A problem on an inspection does not automatically mean the entire system has failed. A broken baffle or a cracked line is often a repair. A failing drain field is a bigger conversation. Action Septic's advice to buyers and sellers in North Fulton is to get a clear look — and, if work is needed, an estimate after someone has seen the site, because every property is different.

Questions homeowners ask

Do you install new septic systems in Alpharetta?

Yes. Installations, along with tank, line, and drain field replacement, are listed among Action Septic Tank Service's official services. The company is based in the Alpharetta-Milton area.

Is a new drain field always required if the yard is wet?

Not always. Standing water after a storm can be temporary. The company looks for signs that persist after the yard dries, and for problems in the tank, lines, or distribution box that might be repaired instead.
