The house

A gated single-family home with a wide back lawn, a covered patio, and a bright sunroom lined with windows. Inside are wood floors, a dining table by the window, and a tiled sitting nook. The photos below are the ones published on the home's senior-living directory listing.

What the license says

  • Memory care: CDSS data lists this home as serving residents with dementia. Ask how the home supports memory care day to day, including supervision, routines and safety.
  • Non-ambulatory: six residents, all of whom may be non-ambulatory.
  • Hospice: state license notes list a hospice waiver covering up to six residents.

Inspection record

The most recent state visit listed is November 5, 2025. Public summaries of the CDSS data checked in November 2026 show no Type A or Type B citations and no complaint visits for this license. How to read the state file.

A note on the address

Some directories file this home under 92120 or under Grantville. The state license lists it at 6140 Crawford Street, San Diego, CA 92123, in Serra Mesa. If you're mapping a visit, use 92123. The CDSS record lists a separate phone for this home, (619) 929-1730. The main Vibrant Living line, (619) 971-8431, is listed for tours of either home.

The neighborhood

Serra Mesa sits on the mesa between Mission Valley and Kearny Mesa, with quick access to I-15, I-805 and SR-163. The Kearny Mesa medical cluster, including Sharp Memorial Hospital and Kaiser Permanente San Diego Medical Center, is close by.
