Tour checklist

How to choose a board and care home

You're choosing the people as much as the house. This checklist helps you see both.

Call (619) 971-8431Schedule a tourDirections

Before you visit

  1. Look up the license on the CDSS care facility search. Confirm it's licensed, the capacity, and the address.
  2. Read the last few inspection and complaint reports. How to read them.
  3. Check whether the home has the waivers your loved one may need: hospice, non-ambulatory clearance, dementia care.

Questions about care

  • How many caregivers are on duty during the day and overnight? Are night staff awake?
  • What training do caregivers have, especially in dementia care?
  • How are medications stored, given and recorded?
  • Who do you call when someone falls or gets sick at night?
  • How is a care plan written, and how often is it updated?
  • What would make you ask a resident to move out?

What to look for on the tour

  • Smell and cleanliness: a home should smell like a home.
  • How caregivers talk to residents: by name, at eye level, without rushing.
  • Residents: dressed, groomed, engaged or comfortable.
  • Safety: grab bars, clear walkways, how doors are secured for residents who wander.
  • Food: ask to see the menu, or visit at mealtime.
  • The outdoors: a safe yard or patio residents actually use.

After the tour

Ask for the admission agreement and the rate in writing, and read them before deciding. Visit a second time at a different time of day if you can. A good home won't mind.
