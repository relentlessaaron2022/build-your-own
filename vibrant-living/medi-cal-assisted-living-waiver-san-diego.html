Paying for care

The Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver in San Diego

The Assisted Living Waiver (ALW) is a Medi-Cal program that helps pay for care in participating assisted living and board and care homes. San Diego County is one of the participating counties.

What the ALW pays for, and what it doesn't

The waiver pays the home for assisted living services: personal care, supervision and related help. It does not pay for room and board. The resident pays room and board separately, typically from their own income, at an amount set by program rules. That split is the most common surprise for families.

Who may qualify

In general, applicants must have full-scope Medi-Cal, need a nursing-facility level of care, and be willing to live in a participating home in a participating county. The California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) runs the program and sets the current rules, so check DHCS before relying on any summary, including this one.

How it works: Care Coordination Agencies

  1. A family contacts a Care Coordination Agency (CCA) that serves San Diego County.
  2. The CCA assesses the applicant and confirms eligibility.
  3. If a waiver slot is available, the CCA helps place the person in a participating home and writes a care plan. If not, the applicant may go on a waitlist.
  4. The CCA stays involved, checking that care matches the plan.

Vibrant Living and the ALW

The Lance Place home is listed as accepting the Assisted Living Waiver on Choose Well San Diego and appears on a DHCS-derived ALW provider list checked in September 2026. A listing is not an open waiver bed. Call (619) 971-8431 to ask whether an ALW room is available now, and whether the Crawford Street home participates.

If you're starting from zero

  • Confirm your loved one's Medi-Cal status first.
  • Ask the CCA about current waitlist times.
  • Ask each home what the resident's room-and-board share would be.
