Are the Vibrant Living homes licensed?

Yes. Both are licensed by the California Department of Social Services as Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly: Lance Place is #374604775 and Crawford Street is #374604608.

How many residents live in each home?

Each home is licensed for up to six residents.

Where are the homes?

6236 Lance Place, San Diego, CA 92120, and 6140 Crawford Street, San Diego, CA 92123 (Serra Mesa).

Can a resident receive hospice care and stay in the home?

State license notes list a hospice waiver at both homes, so residents can receive care from a licensed hospice agency while staying in the home. Ask about current availability.

Do the homes accept residents who use a wheelchair?

Both homes' fire clearances allow up to six non-ambulatory residents. Visit to confirm the room and bathroom layout suit your loved one's equipment.

Do you accept the Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver?

The Lance Place home is listed as accepting the ALW. A listing does not mean a waiver bed is open, so call to ask.

Does either home provide memory care?

CDSS data lists the Crawford Street home as serving residents with dementia. Ask how each home supports memory care day to day.

What languages are spoken?

The Lance Place profile on Choose Well San Diego lists English and Spanish.

How much does it cost?

Rates depend on the room and the level of care. Call (619) 971-8431 for current rates; online directory figures are often outdated.

How do I schedule a tour?

Call (619) 971-8431 or email vibrantlivingsd@gmail.com.
