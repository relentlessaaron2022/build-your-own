How to read a CDSS inspection report

Every licensed board and care home in California has a public file. Ten minutes with it tells you more than any brochure.

Call (619) 971-8431Schedule a tourDirections

Where to find it

  1. Go to the CDSS care facility search.
  2. Search by facility name, number or address.
  3. Open the facility, then its reports: inspections, complaint investigations and other visits.

Kinds of visits

  • Routine or required inspections: generally unannounced visits by a Licensing Program Analyst.
  • Complaint investigations: visits after someone files a complaint. Each has an outcome.
  • Other visits: follow-ups, case management, and checks that corrections were made.

Type A vs Type B citations

Type AType B
A deficiency that poses an immediate risk to residents' health, safety or personal rights.A deficiency that, if not corrected, could become a risk.

A Type B for a missing form is not the same as a Type A for unsafe care. Read the narrative, not only the count.

Complaint outcomes

  • Substantiated: the allegation was found to be true.
  • Inconclusive: not enough evidence either way.
  • Unfounded: found to be false or without basis.

What to look for

  • Patterns: the same problem cited more than once.
  • How fast deficiencies were corrected.
  • Whether citations concern care (medications, supervision, falls) or paperwork.
  • How recent they are, and whether the administrator has changed since.
