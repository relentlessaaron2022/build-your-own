The house

Lance Place is a single-family home. The Choose Well San Diego profile lists three bedrooms and two bathrooms, which is why families may be offered a private or a shared room. The photos below show a living room that opens to the yard, a white galley kitchen with a dining table, and a shaded patio with benches facing a garden and hillside view.

What the license allows

  • Non-ambulatory residents: fire clearance for up to six non-ambulatory residents.
  • Hospice: a hospice waiver (state notes list up to three residents). A resident on hospice can stay in the home with a licensed hospice agency.
  • Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver: listed as an ALW home on Choose Well and on a DHCS-derived ALW list checked in September 2026. A listing is not an open waiver bed. Ask about current availability.
  • Languages: English and Spanish.

Inspection record

The most recent state visit listed is May 12, 2026. Public summaries of the CDSS data checked in November 2026 show no Type A or Type B citations for this license. How to read the state file yourself.

The neighborhood

92120 covers Del Cerro, Allied Gardens, San Carlos and Grantville, a quiet residential part of the city near Lake Murray and Mission Trails Regional Park. For families on this side of San Diego, visiting can fit into a normal day. Hospitals in the area include Alvarado Hospital and Kaiser Permanente's Zion Medical Center.

How Lance Place differs from Crawford Street

Both homes are six beds under the same operator. Lance Place is in 92120 and is the home listed for the Assisted Living Waiver. Crawford Street is in Serra Mesa (92123), near Kearny Mesa. Some directories still list an old Vibrant Living address on Bounty Street (closed in early 2025) or one in La Mesa. Lance Place is the correct 92120 home.
