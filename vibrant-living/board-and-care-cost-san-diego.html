What board and care costs in San Diego, and how to read a quote

Online directories rarely agree on what a small home charges. This page explains why, and how to get a number you can actually plan around.

Why directory prices don't match

If you search a small San Diego home, you may see one price on one directory, a much higher "starting at" figure on a national referral site, and a third number labeled an estimate. Those figures come from different places: what the home reported at some point, a site's regional average, or a modeled estimate. Some are years old. None is a quote. Vibrant Living gives current rates by phone.

What moves the monthly rate

  1. Private vs shared room. Usually the biggest factor in a small home.
  2. Level of care. Two-person transfers, incontinence care, dementia supervision and frequent night help take more staff time.
  3. Hospice or higher-support needs.
  4. Special diets such as pureed food or thickened liquids.

Ways families pay

  • Private funds, including the sale or rental of a home.
  • Long-term care insurance (check the policy's coverage for residential care).
  • Veterans benefits such as Aid and Attendance for eligible veterans and spouses.
  • The Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver, which pays for care services (not room and board) in participating homes.

Medicare generally does not pay for board and care rent or long-term custodial care.
