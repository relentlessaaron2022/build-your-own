Marketing

How to market a 6-bed home without looking like a campus

Families rarely find small homes on broad searches like “assisted living San Diego.” They find them through neighborhood searches, directories and referrals, and those listings are often wrong.

Not legal advice. This is a plain-language overview for operators. Requirements change, so confirm everything with the CDSS Community Care Licensing Division, the current Title 22 regulations, and your own advisors.

1. Claim and fix your Google Business Profile

  • One profile per licensed house, with the exact licensed address.
  • The right category, phone number and website.
  • Hours that make sense for a 24-hour home. Office or touring hours are fine if they're labeled.
  • Real photos of each house: living room, a bedroom, the kitchen, the yard.

2. Audit every directory

Placement and review sites copy each other and rarely update themselves. Search every site for your home's name and phone number, and look for closed addresses, wrong ZIP codes, the other house's details on this house's page, and prices that were never yours. Claim and correct them, or ask the site to.

3. Finish your Choose Well profile

In San Diego County, Choose Well San Diego profiles carry weight with families and professionals. A complete profile with photos, languages, waivers and contact details beats a thin one.

4. Earn honest reviews

Ask families who are happy with your care to describe it in their own words on Google or Yelp. Don't offer anything in return, and never write reviews yourself.

5. Publish a real website

A site that loads, with a page for each house, the license numbers, the waivers you hold and a phone number that's answered, is what turns a directory click into a tour. An expired security certificate or a "site not found" page sends families to the next home.

6. Target the searches you can win

"6-bed board and care," your neighborhood plus "board and care," your ZIP code, and specific needs such as hospice or non-ambulatory care. Large campuses can't honestly own those searches. A small home can.
