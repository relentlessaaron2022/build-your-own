Who this is for

Most of California's RCFEs are small houses, and most small-home owners learn the job by doing it. These pages collect the basics in plain language, with links to the official sources: the CDSS Community Care Licensing Division and Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations.

Not legal advice. This is a plain-language overview for operators. Requirements change, so confirm everything with the CDSS Community Care Licensing Division, the current Title 22 regulations, and your own advisors.
