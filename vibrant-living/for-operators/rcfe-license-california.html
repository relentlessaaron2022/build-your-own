Licensing

How to get an RCFE license in California

A license to operate a Residential Care Facility for the Elderly comes from the California Department of Social Services, Community Care Licensing Division (CCLD). The path has several pieces that run in parallel.

Not legal advice. This is a plain-language overview for operators. Requirements change, so confirm everything with the CDSS Community Care Licensing Division, the current Title 22 regulations, and your own advisors.

1. Attend the licensing orientation

CCLD requires prospective licensees to complete its orientation for the facility type before applying. It explains the application packet, timelines and common reasons applications are delayed.

2. Get an administrator certificate

Every RCFE needs a certified administrator. That means completing an approved initial certification training program, passing the state exam, and clearing a background check, then renewing the certificate on schedule with continuing education. Check CDSS for the current hour requirements. The licensee and the administrator can be the same person.

3. Line up the house and the fire clearance

The home needs a fire clearance from the fire authority, and the number of non-ambulatory or bedridden residents you can accept depends on it. Decide early what population you want to serve, because it affects which house works and what safety features you'll need.

4. Background checks

The licensee, administrator, staff and certain others in the home need criminal record clearance through Live Scan fingerprinting before they have contact with residents.

5. Submit the application packet

Expect to provide the application, a plan of operation, your admission agreement, financial information showing you can operate, staffing plans, a facility sketch, and the fire clearance request. Incomplete packets are the most common cause of delay.

6. Pre-licensing visit

A Licensing Program Analyst visits the house to confirm it matches the application and meets the physical requirements before the license is issued.

Where to start

Begin at the CDSS Community Care Licensing site for the current orientation schedule and application forms.
