What we built

We built 25 websites for our friends at Vibrant Living — two licensed 6-bed board-and-care homes in San Diego, led by Julie Norris. Fourteen pages help families understand the homes, licensing, hospice, Medi-Cal, and how to schedule a tour. Eleven more pages speak to operators who want to start or run a small RCFE.

The family-facing set includes the home page, Lance Place in Del Cerro (license #374604775), Crawford Street in Serra Mesa (license #374604608), guides on six-bed homes versus large campuses, what an RCFE is in California, hospice, non-ambulatory care, how to choose a home, reading CDSS inspection reports, cost, the Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver, an FAQ, a tour page, and a page about Julie Norris and Greater Solutions.

The operator set includes an operators hub, plus pages on starting a board-and-care in San Diego, RCFE licensing, Title 22 for six-bed homes, staffing and caregiver training, medication and care-plan records, CCL inspections, hospice waivers, the Assisted Living Waiver as a provider, marketing a six-bed home, and running two small homes. The work starts at the Vibrant Living home page.
