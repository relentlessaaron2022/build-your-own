About Vibrant Living

A small San Diego operator that has chosen to stay small: two six-bed homes, one administrator, and more than ten years in residential care.

The operator

Both Vibrant Living homes are licensed to Greater Solutions, a San Diego company registered for residential assisted living, and the state license record lists Julie Norris as administrator of both. The first Vibrant Living home was licensed in San Diego in January 2014. The Crawford Street home has been licensed since 2022 and Lance Place since 2024.

Why two small homes instead of one big one

Growing a care business usually means adding beds under one roof. Vibrant Living grew by adding a second house. That keeps each home at six residents, where caregivers can know every person well, while giving families a choice of two neighborhoods and two different houses.

What the homes say they focus on

  • Helping each resident reach the highest level of functioning they can.
  • Social stimulation and planned activities based on residents' interests.
  • Attention to diet.
  • A supportive, home-like setting.

Licensed and on the record

Lance Place is CDSS RCFE #374604775 and Crawford Street is #374604608. Both are on file with Community Care Licensing, and the Lance Place home has a profile on Choose Well San Diego. How to check any home's record.
