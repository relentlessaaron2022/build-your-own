Helpful to share when you call

  • Your loved one's age and current living situation.
  • What help they need: bathing, dressing, medications, mobility, memory.
  • Whether they use a wheelchair or walker, or are on or near hospice.
  • How you plan to pay, including whether you're asking about the Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver.
  • How soon you need a move-in.

On the visit

Walk the home, see the available room, meet the caregivers and ask anything. Bring the tour checklist. There's no payment or online application on this site. Admissions happen in person, with a written agreement.
