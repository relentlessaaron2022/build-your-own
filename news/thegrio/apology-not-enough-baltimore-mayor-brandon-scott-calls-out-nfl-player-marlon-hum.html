‘Apology not enough’: Baltimore Mayor Brandon Scott calls out NFL player Marlon Humphrey after comment saying city makes the ‘best criminals’

Baltimore Ravens player Marlon Humphrey apologized Wednesday after joking on his podcast that Baltimore makes the 'best criminals.' Mayor Brandon Scott said the apology is not enough, arguing the words hurt young Black boys who look up to him.

Source: TheGrio · Published Nov 18, 2026, 9:54 PM ET · Read the full story →

“Baltimore is a city full of young Black boys who look to the Ravens as a beacon of light in their lives and to have someone that looks like them who wears a Baltimore Ravens jersey relegate them to becoming criminals cuts deeper.”— Mayor Brandon Scott

The details

Scott invited Humphrey to sit down with community members who were formerly incarcerated and now help others avoid the same mistakes. Humphrey removed the episode from his YouTube channel.
