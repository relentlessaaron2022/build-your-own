A Song for a Lost Love, A Memory Unforgotten

A raw, poignant tale of love lost and found in the streets of Atlanta. Every beat a memory, every lyric a heartache.

By Relentless AaronNovember 1, 2026Read the original at relentlessaaron.net

The Scar: A Lost Love

It was a cold winter night, the kind where the wind cuts like a knife. My lover, the one who had been my everything, was gone. We parted ways under a bridge, the city lights reflecting in her eyes, the finality of our goodbye echoing in the silence.

The Alibi Is the Music

I found her there, in the midst of the night, under the same bridge where our story had begun and ended. The song “The Alibi Is the Music” by Relentless Aaron played softly in my ears, a bittersweet reminder of what could have been. The beat was slow, the melody mournful, and every word hit like a punch to the gut.

I stood there, listening to her, the song, the city, and the memory of us. The lyrics painted a picture of a love that once burned bright but now lay cold and forgotten. The track starts with a spoken word, “I never meant to hurt you, I just… I lost control,” and it was like a punch in the chest, the truth of it hitting harder than any punch ever could.

The verses tell the story of a love that was real, that was everything, and how it all came crashing down. The chorus repeats, “The alibi is the music, I made a mistake, I can’t go back to how it was,” and with each repetition, the memory of her fades just a bit more.

The Scene: A Memory Unforgotten

The song takes me back to that night, the bridge, the rain, and the cold. We stood there, just the two of us, under the same sky, the same stars, the same city. The lyrics describe the moment, the feel of her in my arms, the taste of the kiss that could have been our future.

“Under this bridge, you and me, we were a match made in heaven, now I’m just a liar,” the song whispers, and I can hear her voice in my head, the way she used to speak, the way she used to laugh. Each line is a memory, a moment, a piece of her that I carry with me.

The instrumental builds, the beat picks up, but the heartache remains. It’s a song of loss, a song of memory, a song of love that once was but now is gone. The track ends with a soft piano note, a finality that lingers, a reminder that some memories are best left alone.
