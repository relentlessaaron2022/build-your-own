Emory Jones: Resource, Respect, Remembering… - Home of Relentless Aaron

A remembrance piece for Emory Jones, framed around resource, respect, and memory (full body on the 2016 Wayback capture).

By Relentless AaronNovember 23, 2007Read the original at relentlessaaron.com

Nov

23

Uncategorized

I just received a letter from my buddy Emory (along with the photo) and I was brought to write this BLOG with respect to how important this man was in my life.

We were both locked up in Fort Dix, NJ and outside of his routine card games (and my routine writing) we spent much quality time together. It was a growing relationship even though I was mostly out of sight, buried in my writing somewhere. We’ grub together, walk and talk together, and eventually I learned that Emory was Jay Z’s cousin. Funny thing is, when “E” brought it up, that “I’m hooked up with one of the biggest clothing companies in the world,” I sort of shrugged it off. 1st of all, Emory wasn’t bragging, but his mentioning such a thing had no relevance insofar as our relationship was concerned. It took a lot to impress me during those tough times in prison. And 2nd, I was immune to cats telling me they knew this person and that person, and that they owned this ‘n that. Now, E didn’t come across that way; in fact, his mentioning this was a part of an ongoing conversation as he came to recognize my business savvy and discipline. Bottom line is, I thought that I was the biggest thing happening on “the compound” where it relates to talent and business combined, not to mention my helluva softball game. (More on that MUCH later) Meanwhile, E eventually whipped out a picture of him Dame, Jay, and Biggs. They were toasting at a dinner table, and by the looks of it they were ballin big time. Still, a nodded with that smidgen of doubt, since I knew hat most anyone can get a picture with a celeb, especially if you knew the right people, or if you happen to be part of the “in crowd.” What I didn’t know was how deep the E/Dame/Jay relationship was. And frankly, it still didn’t matter. E was my buddy, and we were experiencing TWS. (Time Well Spent.)

Furthermore, E was a philanthropist! He asked me what I needed to make my writing agenda comfortable. That meant commissary, money in my account, whatever. Emory did this with no strings attached, no contracts or binding agreements. He simply said to me, “I feel like you’re the Michael Jordan of this book game. And I wanna see you blow up.”

So week after week, E would use much of his “commissary spending limit” to accommodate me so that I was comfortable under the circumstances. He had that leap of faith that I’ve very rarely seen, but for my friends Steven Juliano, Tiny Wood and my sister. In my books you always see Emory Jones credited in the acknowledgements because he helped to make this possible. Just as (I’m sure) he had something to do with the early growth of Jay Z, E has also played a starring role in my life. So this BLOG I dedicate to Emory Jones who’s at the end of a long Federal Prison sentence. Thanks Emory. I’ll never forget you. WORD
