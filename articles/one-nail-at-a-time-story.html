One Nail at a Time, My Story of Persistence

A raw look at how one song, “One Nail at a Time,” reflects my journey of overcoming obstacles.

By Relentless AaronOctober 24, 2026Read the original at relentlessaaron.net

Every stream buys back a piece of what it cost.

The Day I Lost My Job

The sun barely peeked over the Atlanta skyline when my boss called. “Your services are no longer required,” he said, his voice as flat as a pancake. I hung up, my heart heavy, and walked to my car, leaving behind a steady paycheck.

One Nail at a Time

I parked my car in the dimly lit lot of my old stomping grounds, the neighborhood where I grew up. “One Nail at a Time” by Relentless Aaron played on my car stereo. The lyrics echoed in the empty streets:

> One nail at a time, I rise > One step at a time, I thrive

I parked the car, leaned back, and let the words wash over me. Each verse reminded me of the small victories that kept me going, the incremental steps that built my resilience.

The Neighborhood

Walking up the cracked sidewalk, I passed the old barber shop where I used to work. It was empty now, the chairs and scissors gathering dust. I passed the corner where I used to hang out, the same one where I often met up with old friends. The park was quiet, the swings and slides untouched. Each place I passed was a reminder of the life I once knew.

Building Resilience

I walked to my house, the same one I grew up in, the walls still holding the memories of my childhood. Inside, my room was in disarray, books scattered on the floor, clothes strewn about. I sat down at my desk, picked up a notebook, and started to write. The words flowed, each sentence a reflection of the battles I’d fought and the ones that lay ahead.

The Future

As I sat there, the song played on, its rhythm a constant reminder of the path I was on. “One nail at a time, I rise,” it sang. I looked out the window, the city below, bustling with people. Each one of them had their own story, their own struggles.

End of the Day

I closed my notebook, put on my jacket, and stepped out into the night. The song “One Nail at a Time” played one last time, its melody a beacon of hope. I knew I had a long road ahead, but I was determined to keep moving forward, one nail at a time.

—

You’ve been paying the cost of not knowing long enough.
