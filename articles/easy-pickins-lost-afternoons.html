A Song for Yesterday’s Lost Afternoons

Relive the carefree days when you could steal hours without a care. #SoulMusic #ConsciousHipHop #AtlantaMemories

By Relentless AaronSeptember 7, 2026Read the original at relentlessaaron.net

I’m walking through the city, streets lined with memories. The sun is just starting to set, and the air is filled with the sweet scent of fresh-cut grass and the distant sound of laughter. I’m in no rush, just taking it all in, the way I used to.

The song, “Easy Pickins,” is a constant in my playlist. It’s got that slow, smoky soul vibe that seems to come from somewhere deep and warm. I press play and the familiar melody starts to play, weaving through the city.

The Scattered Pieces

The first time I heard it, I was walking home from a hard day at work. The song felt as easy as picking a pocket, but that’s exactly what it felt like. There’s something about that melody that just takes you back, even if it’s just for a moment. I can close my eyes and see those old streets, feel the rough texture of the concrete under my fingers.

The Gentle Beat

The beat is like a gentle push, reminding me of the simple joys that slipped away. It’s the kind of music that whispers to your soul, telling you to slow down, to appreciate the small things. The vocals are soft, almost as if the singer is speaking to you directly, in a language you understand but never fully learned.

The Stream of Memories

I remember the afternoons spent on the porch, listening to the radio, and letting the world pass by. Those were the days when the day was mine to do with as I pleased. Now, the song pulls me back to that place, to those simple, easy pickin’ afternoons.

The Cost of Not Knowing

You’ve been paying the cost of not knowing long enough. Every stream buys back a piece of what it cost. You could have had more of those afternoons, but the cost was always the same.

Bringing It Home

As I walk, the song carries me through the city, taking me to places I’ve forgotten. It’s a song that’s as much about the here and now as it is about the memories. It’s about the simple pleasures that make life worth living, even on the hard days.
