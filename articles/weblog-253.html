My New Slogan for Thanksgiving 2007 - Home of Relentless Aaron

A Thanksgiving 2007 post announcing a new personal slogan (full body on the 2016 Wayback capture).

By Relentless AaronNovember 22, 2007Read the original at relentlessaaron.com

Nov

22

Uncategorized

Nothing ever quite happens as fast as you’d like it to; It’s either too slow to keep up with the bills, or it happens so fast that you miss it. If it’s too slow to keep up with a high maintenance wife, watch out, because she’ll throw you out of her house and keep the kids from you before she turns to her friends that have money, families and businesses. It’s an uphill battle that you don’t wanna fight-so just say “fuck it.” If it’s too slow, the marraige will be over and all hopes for raising a decent family will be scrapped. If it’s too slow, it will test your relationship with friends and family and show you what the term “fair weather” really means.

On the other hand, if it’s too fast you just might miss your destiny, your passion and purpose.

And BTW: Happy Thanksgiving
