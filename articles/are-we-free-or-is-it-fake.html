Are We FREE? Or Is It FAKE?

We love to shout about freedom.Post about it.March for it.Celebrate it.But if we’re being honest — most of us are still stuck.

By Relentless AaronJune 20, 2025Read the original at relentlessaaron.net

By Relentless Aaron

We love to shout about freedom.
Post about it.
March for it.
Celebrate it.
But if we’re being honest — most of us are still stuck.

Not in literal chains.
But bound by old mindsets.
By outdated systems.
By invisible cages we’ve normalized.

✊🏾 Freedom ain’t real if…

  • You don’t know how to talk to your own people without drama.
  • You’re still a slave to a paycheck.
  • You follow trends but never learn tools.
  • You “own” nothing — not your content, not your platform, not your time.
  • You’re too busy reacting to build something real.

We’re calling ourselves free while living out scripts someone else wrote for us.

That’s not liberation. That’s performance.

💥 So What Do We Do?

🔁 1. Unlearn the Generational Traps

Stop repeating what hurt you.
Toxic masculinity, scarcity mindsets, envy, betrayal — we inherited dysfunction like a family heirloom.

We can’t build new futures on that old blueprint.

🧠 2. Learn to Communicate

We shout but don’t speak.
We ghost instead of resolving.
We perform but don’t connect.

Real power lies in emotional intelligence. Clarity. Peaceful conflict resolution.

📚 3. Develop a Discipline for Learning

No more “I’m too old for this tech.”
Learn the tools.
Study the market.
Build real skills in AI, content, automation, credit, marketing.
Education is not optional anymore.

🧱 4. OWN Everything You Can

No more renting access to your own brilliance.
Own your story, your workflow, your brand, your IP.
Freedom without ownership is slavery in Wi-Fi.

🔄 5. REINVEST in What You Built

We blow checks on distractions and call it success.
Put it back in your ecosystem — your gear, your wellness, your peace.
The smartest flex is reinvestment.
