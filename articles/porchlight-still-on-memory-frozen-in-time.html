“Porchlight Still On”, A Memory Frozen In Time

Relive a childhood summer under the glow of an old porch light in this soulful tale.

By Relentless AaronOctober 20, 2026Read the original at relentlessaaron.net

The Light That Guided Me Home

It was 1985, my 7th summer in the neighborhood. Every night, I’d watch the porch light flicker on as my mom called out from inside: “Aaron, time for dinner.” The porchlight wasn’t just a source of light; it was a beacon that marked the end of playtime and the beginning of family dinners.

I remember the first time I saw the light fully lit. It was the night my uncle returned home after serving in Vietnam. He’d been gone almost two years, and his absence had left a void in our family dynamic. As he stepped through the door, the porchlight cast an orange glow over him, making it seem like he’d just walked out of a sunlit field.

The light had a life of its own, flickering softly, as if it too was remembering old times and new ones. It reminded me that even after all those years, some things never changed. My uncle would sit on the front steps, telling stories about the war, and I’d listen from afar, my heart full of both fear and fascination.

Every night, under that light, we’d talk until it was time for bed. The porchlight was a silent witness to our conversations, soaking up the sounds of family life. It wasn’t just a light; it was a symbol of home and safety in a world that felt increasingly uncertain.

The Shadows of Departure

One summer evening, as I stood on the front porch watching my uncle leave for another day’s work, the porchlight flickered out abruptly. A sudden gust of wind from the south had blown some debris into its mechanism. My dad rushed over to fix it, but by then, the light was dimming and wouldn’t come back on.

That night, I lay awake in bed, staring at my own small bedroom window, trying to replicate the warm glow of the porchlight. The house seemed darker without that familiar light, as if the whole world had lost its way. In a sense, it had, my uncle was gone, and with him, part of our family’s history.

The porchlight represented more than just a source of illumination; it held memories of childhood summers, stories told around the dinner table, and the warmth of familial bonds. When that light finally burned out completely, I knew something irreplaceable had been lost.

Reliving A Memory

Years later, when I found “Porchlight Still On,” it felt like an old friend coming back to visit. The track captures those moments so vividly: the soft glow of the porchlight, the sound of my uncle’s return, and the warmth of family gatherings. It’s a song that takes you right back to that summer, with all its light and shadows.

The first time I listened, tears welled up as the music transported me back in time. The lyrics are simple yet powerful: “Porchlight still on, but no one home / Memories echo, they won’t come alone.” These words resonated deeply because of their truth.

One Last Listen

Every stream buys back a piece of what it cost. If you find yourself missing those childhood summers or the warmth of home and family, give “Porchlight Still On” a listen. It’s not just a song; it’s a journey through memory lane.
