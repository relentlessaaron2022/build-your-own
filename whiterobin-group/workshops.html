Acting, Shakespeare, script analysis, and career counseling

WhiteRobin describes acting as doing truthfully under imaginary circumstances, and the actor’s job as creating truthful behavior. The topics below come from their public workshops page. Locations and calendars are posted when a session is announced — including past and possible cities such as New York, New Jersey, Atlanta, Jacksonville, and Orlando.

Workshop topics they publish

These descriptions follow The WhiteRobin Group’s own workshops page. They are not a promise that a given topic is on the calendar this month.

Acting and scene study

Through scene study and exercises, students explore point of view, subtext, meanings, actions, and objectives — tools of the actor — toward truthful behavior and crafted characters. Their site invites actors to spend a few hours on the craft.

Monologues

Find the launching point of the piece, the objective, and the action needed to achieve it, so a familiar audition piece can become an emotionally alive moment of acting.

Shakespeare

How does the actor marry truthful, contemporary behavior to classical verse? Their workshops page describes work on decoding iambic pentameter, playing imagery and blank verse, and “taking the audience along.”

Script analysis

Their premise is direct: anything you do not understand, you cannot act. Students learn to decode scripts, discern cause and effect, understand the playwright’s worldview, and answer the whys of the text.

Dialects

WhiteRobin teaches that dialect creates character, and that class and culture shape rhythm, word order, and word choice. Once those are understood, they say, the dialect adjustment is easier to learn and retain.

Career counseling

They describe this as a change of perspective and as encouragement. See the section below if you are a student, a parent, or an actor considering a move or a program audition.

For students and parents considering theatre programs

Among the career-counseling needs WhiteRobin lists publicly are auditioning for a graduate or undergraduate theatre program, transitioning from musical theatre to straight theatre, moving into the New York City marketplace, and re-energizing a lagging career. They say they are prepared to address those needs, identify goals, and clarify purpose.

That is the parent-facing offering we can confirm. This site does not advertise a children’s acting program, because none is described on their public pages. If you are helping a student prepare college or conservatory auditions, write or call and ask about career counseling or a coaching session.

How dates and places are announced

Workshop locations are posted when the workshops are announced. Their public pages have mentioned Atlanta, Jacksonville, New York, and Orlando, and Angela has spoken about teaching workshops in New Jersey. An older “now offering” page has listed an Atlanta intensive with a price and a 2020s calendar line that does not include a year; that listing is not treated here as an upcoming event, and no price from it is repeated.

The only recent public New York date we can confirm is the one-day intensive of October 24, 2026, whose ticket sales have ended. To hear about the next workshop, use the contact page or their Eventbrite organizer listing.
