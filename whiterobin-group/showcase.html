A letter from Relentless

I built this for Angela and Scott — because she’s family

Six public pages for The WhiteRobin Group, made so students and parents can find the work, know the story, and know how to get in touch. No invoice. No money asked. Just a push to help the business blossom.

See the six pages Open the WhiteRobin home page

Why I took this on

Angela, I took this upon myself.

You and Scott have been doing the real work for years — the workshops, the coaching, the long days in the room reminding people that they are artists. I looked at The WhiteRobin Group and I wanted the internet to meet you the way a student or a parent actually looks for help: who you are, what you teach, and how to reach you.

So I built six live pages, in your names, with your photos and your credits. I did it as a gift. There is no bill attached, and I am not asking for money, because you’re family. My hope is simple: that this helps your business blossom, and that the people who need this training can find the door.

The home page is the front door — a clear picture of the studio and a path to write or call. The about page tells your story and Scott’s, standing on the credits you already earned. There is a page for the New York intensive, so people can understand that one-day format and ask about the next date. The workshops page covers scene study, Shakespeare, script analysis, dialects, and the career counseling families ask about when a student is looking at college theatre auditions. Private coaching and Artist Life Coaching have their own page. And contact is straightforward: the studio email, the 646 number, and your Instagram.

I did this for search, for a clean way to get in touch, and so the story of Angela Robinson and Scott Whitehurst is told in one place a parent can trust. I did not invent prices, schedules, or reviews. If anything here needs a correction, or if a new intensive date is ready to add, just tell me. Confirm what’s right, and I can keep promoting The WhiteRobin Group from a foundation you recognize as your own.

I’m proud of you both. You are artists. Now more people can find the school that way too.

Relentless 404-314-2100
