The WhiteRobin Group · Founded 2008

Acting workshops and coaching with two working professionals

Angela Robinson and Scott Whitehurst train actors in scene study, Shakespeare, script analysis, dialects, and the practical work of building a career. Their tagline is simple: you are an Artist.

A studio for students, parents, and working actors

WhiteRobin is not a large conservatory with a year-round storefront catalog. It is a teaching practice run by two working actors. They announce intensives and workshops by city, and they offer private coaching by appointment. Families looking at undergraduate or graduate theatre auditions can also ask about the career counseling they list on their site: help preparing for those program auditions, thinking through a move into the New York marketplace, or re-energizing a career already underway.

Coaching details

Train the gift. Turn it into technique. Tie excellence to purpose.

That three-part mission is how WhiteRobin describes its work: train the actor’s gift, transform talent into technique, and connect excellence to a clear purpose. The practice has been teaching since 2008, with announced workshops in cities that have included New York, New Jersey, Atlanta, Jacksonville, and Orlando.
