What to mention in your note

A short email is enough. It helps them if you say whether you are writing about:

  • The next New York acting intensive
  • A workshop topic such as Shakespeare, scene study, or dialects
  • Private coaching or Artist Life Coaching with Angela
  • Career counseling for undergraduate or graduate theatre-program auditions, a move to New York, or a career transition

Please do not expect this page to quote a fee. Prices are confirmed by the studio when you reach them, or on an Eventbrite listing when a new event is posted.

For parents

If you are a parent helping a student look at serious acting study, start with the teachers’ credits and training and the career-counseling language on the workshops page. WhiteRobin publicly offers help with graduate and undergraduate theatre-program auditions. They have not published a separate children’s curriculum on the pages reviewed for this site, so we do not advertise kids’ classes here. If you have a question about age or readiness, ask them.
