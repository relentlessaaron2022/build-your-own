Who this is for

The intensive is aimed at actors who want concentrated time in the room with Angela and Scott — students building a craft, working professionals preparing the next role, and parents helping a young adult think about serious training. WhiteRobin has not published a children’s program or a minimum age on the public pages reviewed for this site, so we do not describe this as a kids’ class. If you have a question about whether a particular student is a good fit, ask them directly.

If you want ongoing work rather than a single day, look at their workshop topics and private coaching.
