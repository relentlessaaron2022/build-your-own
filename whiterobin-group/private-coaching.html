One-on-one, by appointment

Private acting coaching with Angela or Scott

The WhiteRobin Group invites actors to book a one-hour coaching session with Scott or Angela, or a one-hour Artist Life Coaching session with Angela. A public booking calendar was not found, so the way to start is to email or call the studio.

Email to inquire Call (646) 308-1366

What they say coaching is for

On their workshops page, private coaching is described in two parts. This page stays with that language and does not add unpublished packages or fees.

Audition preparation

Preparation for stage, film, or television. They write that by making strong and specific choices, you will use your monologues or sides effectively and work toward the callback.

Role preparation

Break down the text, craft the role, and work toward characters that are vibrant, truthful, and alive — the wording they use on their own site.

Artist Life Coaching with Angela

Alongside craft coaching, their homepage offers a one-hour Artist Life Coaching session with Angela Robinson and mentions an Artist Life Coaching Series. No public schedule, syllabus, or price for the series was available when this page was built. If that is what you are looking for, say so when you write or call.

Students and parents who are thinking about conservatory or college theatre auditions may also want the career counseling they list for graduate and undergraduate program auditions. That conversation can live in a coaching hour or a counseling session; they can tell you which is the better fit.

How to request a session

Email whiterobingroup@gmail.com or call (646) 308-1366. Tell them whether you want to work with Angela, with Scott, or are open to either, and whether the session is for audition prep, role prep, Artist Life Coaching, or college-audition counseling. Rates are not published on this site.

If you would rather start in a group room, read about the New York intensive and other workshops, then use the contact page.
