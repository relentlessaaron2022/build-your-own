## Reel Concepts

Each concept runs 20 to 35 seconds, vertical 9:16, captions burned in. Cut on motion. Hook is always in the first two seconds.

**Reel 1 — "The Stairwell" (Push, Day 1)**
1. 0–2s: Tight shot, a hand pushing open a heavy stairwell door. Text: "Page one."
2. 2–6s: Handheld walk up concrete steps, low light, footsteps loud.
3. 6–14s: Voiceover reads the first lines of Push [AARON: approve excerpt]. Text on screen in sync.
4. 14–20s: Stop on a landing. A phone buzzes off camera. Freeze.
5. 20–25s: Cut to book cover on black. Text: "Find out who's calling. Link in bio." [LINK]

**Reel 2 — "Keys" (Sugar Daddy universe, Day 4)**
1. 0–2s: Car keys dropped onto a glass table, sharp sound. Text: "He paid for everything."
2. 2–6s: Slow push in on a woman's hand not picking them up.
3. 6–12s: Three hard cuts: an apartment door, a receipt, a phone face down. One word each: "Car." "Condo." "Secret."
4. 12–18s: Phone flips over. Screen lights up. We don't see the name.
5. 18–25s: Cover reveal. Text: "Every bill comes due." Link card. [LINK]

**Reel 3 — "Page to Beat" (music with Bobby Monroe + Atlanta Music Alliance, Day 6)**
