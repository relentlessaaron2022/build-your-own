## Revenue Levers

Some money moves in days. Some takes the whole quarter. Know which is which, and pull the fast ones first so the slow ones have air.

### The three fastest levers

1. **Signed *Push* series box, sold direct.** You already own the books and the name. A signed box ships from your own store, you keep the margin, and a single email plus three posts can open orders inside a week. Target: 150 boxes, $8,850.
2. **Live online workshop.** One date, one landing page, one Zoom link. Seats sell on urgency, and the content already lives in your head. Target: 60 seats, $5,820, run in Week 4 and again in Week 10.
3. **Merch drop tied to a catalogue world.** A "Last Kingpin" or "Push" tee printed on demand needs no inventory and can go live in 72 hours. Target: 300 tees and 100 hoodies, $17,000 across the quarter.

### The steady middle

- **Ebook and print sales** rise when the fast levers send traffic. Every box buyer, workshop student and tee buyer sees the full catalogue on the thank-you page.
- **Audiobook** is a quiet earner. Push the audio edition to readers who say they "don't have time to read." [VERIFY: which titles already have audio editions]
- **Streams** grow slowly. Treat them as reach first and money second. Pair every new track with a book scene so fans of one find the other. Lift the circle here: feature CoCo Kity and Bobby Monroe tracks on shared playlists, and give Atlanta Music Alliance artists a slot in each drop.

### The slow levers that pay big

- **Sync licensing.** Pitch music to TV, film, games and ad agencies. It takes weeks to land a placement. Start the pitches in Week 2 so money arrives by Week 9 to 13.
- **Speaking.** Schools, reentry programs, libraries and writing conferences book months ahead. Send the pitch now. The non-fiction voice of DeWitt Gilmore leads these talks.

## Cross-Sells

The cheapest sale is the second one. The reader is already holding your book. Hand them the next one.

- **Ebook to bundle.** Every ebook back page ends with a link to the full universe bundle at a reader-only price.
- **Signed box to merch.** At checkout, offer the matching tee for $25 instead of $35. Target an attach rate of 20% [VERIFY], which is 30 tees on 150 boxes.
- **Print to audio.** Insert a card in every direct print order: "Hear it in the car." Link to the audio edition.
- **Workshop to course.** Workshop students get a 48-hour offer on the self-paced course. If 15 of 60 students upgrade [VERIFY], that is 15 x $197 = $2,955 of the course target.
- **Music to books.** Each track page and video caption names the book whose world it belongs to. Fans of the sound walk into the story.
- **Books to music.** Each book's landing page carries a playlist built from the circle: CoCo Kity, Bobby Monroe and Atlanta Music Alliance artists. "Street Stories" pairs with the fiction catalogue.
- **Speaking to everything.** Every talk ends at a table with signed books, a course card and a QR code. Price a school bulk order of 30 paperbacks at $14 each [VERIFY], which is $420 per event.
- **Fiction to non-fiction.** Readers who love the Relentless Aaron stories hear the real-world lessons from DeWitt Gilmore. Keep the two names on separate pages, and link them with one plain line: "Want the playbook behind the stories?"
