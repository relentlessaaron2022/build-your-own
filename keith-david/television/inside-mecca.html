  1. 1956–1979 — Harlem, Queens, Juilliard

    Keith David Williams was born June 4, 1956, in Harlem, Upper Manhattan, and raised in Corona, Queens, according to Wikipedia. The same article, citing a published profile, names his mother, Dolores (née Dickenson), a manager at New York Telephone, and his father, Lester Williams, a director of payroll operations. Wikipedia says he decided to act after playing the Cowardly Lion in a school production of The Wizard of Oz, studied at the High School of Performing Arts, and graduated from the Juilliard School’s Drama Division (Group 8, 1975–1979) with a BFA in 1979.

    Wikipedia

  2. 1980–1982 — The Acting Company to The Thing

    Wikipedia says that in 1980–81 he toured with John Houseman’s The Acting Company in A Midsummer Night’s Dream and Beckett’s Waiting for Godot, and that less than two years later he starred as Childs, opposite Kurt Russell, in John Carpenter’s The Thing (1982).

    Wikipedia

  3. 1986–2004 — Film after film

    Wikipedia’s overview highlights Platoon (1986), They Live (1988), Dead Presidents (1995), Armageddon and There’s Something About Mary (1998), Requiem for a Dream and Pitch Black (2000), Barbershop (2002), and Crash and The Chronicles of Riddick (2004), among many other titles listed on the filmography page.

    Wikipedia On screen and stage

  4. Voice, games, and documentaries

    Signature voice roles listed by Wikipedia include Goliath in Gargoyles, Al Simmons / Spawn in Todd McFarlane’s Spawn, Dr. Facilier in The Princess and the Frog, and the Cat in Coraline. Game credits include the Arbiter in the Halo series, Captain Anderson in Mass Effect, and others on the filmography page. Wikipedia says he won an Emmy Award for Outstanding Voice-Over Performance for narrating Ken Burns’s Unforgivable Blackness: The Rise and Fall of Jack Johnson, and also narrated Burns’s The War. IMDb’s biography, which is a self-serve profile, says he has three Emmys across Burns’s Jackie Robinson, The War, and Unforgivable Blackness. This hub records the Wikipedia-cited Emmy and notes the IMDb claim of three rather than silently choosing.

    Wikipedia IMDb

  5. Stage, later television, and a star

    Wikipedia, citing Variety, records Broadway work including August Wilson’s Seven Guitars and a Tony Award nomination for Jelly’s Last Jam. Later television listed in the overview includes Elroy Patashnik on Community (season 6) and Bishop James Greenleaf on Greenleaf (2016–20). Wikipedia states that on June 4, 2026, he received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. His official site, last updated in 2025, had said he “will be honored in 2026” with that star. An independent ceremony recap was not attached here; the date is recorded as Wikipedia publishes it.
