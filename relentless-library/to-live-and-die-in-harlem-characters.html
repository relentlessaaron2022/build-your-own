Characters in the novel To Live and Die in Harlem

Push’s household and business

Verified notes name girlfriend Yvette (vice president of the company), sister Crystal (a board / silent partner), and nephew Reggie, 19, who helps with renovations. The company name is Melrah — Harlem spelled backwards. Early completed buildings on the block include tenants the notes name: Horace Silvers, and Denise Cosby and Sheryl Roberts on an upper floor. This page lists them because they are in the photographed text, not to invent subplots.

People around the missing-reporter thread

Michelle Scott is a Times reporter who vanishes in the book’s cold open. Judge Charles Pullman and U.S. Probation Officer Evelyn Watson, who is engaged to the judge’s son Peter, discuss her disappearance at Copeland’s restaurant. Other names appear in the judge’s account of connected deaths; those are claims inside the fiction. This draft does not treat them as real cases.

Questions

Is Yvette in Push?

She is documented in the sequel notes. This page does not claim she appears in the first novel without a full collation.

Unknown. The musical does not yet have a verified full album or published libretto in the sources used here.
