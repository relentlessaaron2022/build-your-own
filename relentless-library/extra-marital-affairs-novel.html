Extra Marital Affairs by Relentless Aaron

The official printed title is Extra Marital Affairs — three words — a St. Martin’s Griffin trade paperback from September 5, 2006. Relentless has also referred to it as Extramarital Affairs. The book is adult urban fiction about a married couple whose involvement with a third person ends in her unexpected death, after which they learn she was not who she claimed.

The premise, kept non-explicit

Mason and Adena Fickle are a married couple. Public listings say they become involved with a woman named Loween, who is later found dead, and that they then discover she was not who or what she had said she was. Trouble from the street reaches their door. The question the listings pose is whether the marriage can hold when urgent, dangerous decisions arrive. This page does not describe sexual or violent scenes. Relentless confirmed the third person’s name is Loween.

A Griffin book, not a later reprint of a Relentless Content title we found

Unlike Push and Sugar Daddy, the public record for this title starts at St. Martin’s Griffin in 2006. Addall and Gale both list it that way. No earlier Relentless Content ISBN for Extra Marital Affairs was confirmed in this pass.
