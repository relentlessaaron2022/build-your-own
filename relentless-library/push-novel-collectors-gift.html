Finding a copy of Push to keep or give

Push is easier to gift if you shop by ISBN. The 2007 St. Martin’s paperback is the copy most stores still list. The earlier Relentless Content paperback is the one collectors usually mean when they say “the first edition.”

Give the mass-market copy if you want a readable gift

For a reader who wants the story, the St. Martin’s Paperbacks edition is the practical choice. Check the title page for Relentless Aaron and the ISBN 978-0-312-94969-3 so you do not grab a different book also titled Push.

If you are buying for a shelf, inspect the copy

Early Relentless Content paperbacks can be scarce and may be worn. Confirm the copyright page, the Mount Vernon publisher line, and the paperback ISBN before paying a collector price. This draft does not quote current used prices; they change.
