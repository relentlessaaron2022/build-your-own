Push ISBN, editions, and where to buy

If you are hunting down a copy of Push, you want the ISBN, not just the title. Two editions are documented. A hardcover ISBN printed on the early copyright page conflicts with another title in Open Library, so it is flagged, not treated as confirmed.

The Relentless Content paperback

The early PDF’s copyright page prints Paperback ISBN 0-9670542-1-4 and a publication arrangement with McRae Enterprises. Open Library records a Relentless Content Push dated December 19, 2003, ISBN-13 9780967054216, at 311 pages. That is the first edition we can document.

The St. Martin’s Paperbacks edition

Open Library and bookstore catalogs agree on a St. Martin’s Paperbacks edition dated February 6, 2007, ISBN-13 9780312949693 / ISBN-10 0312949693, list price originally $6.99. Page counts disagree: Open Library says 336 pages; some retail listings say 297. Treat the page count as unreconciled.

A hardcover ISBN we cannot confirm

The same Relentless Content copyright page prints Hardcover ISBN 0-9670542-2-2. Open Library assigns that ISBN to Triple Threat (Relentless Content, July 2004), not to a Push hardcover. Until Relentless or a physical hardcover can settle this, do not use 0-9670542-2-2 as a confirmed Push identifier.

Questions

Which edition should I buy?

If you want the widely listed mass-market copy, look for ISBN 9780312949693. Collectors of the original Relentless Content paperback should search ISBN 0-9670542-1-4 and confirm the title page before paying a premium.

Is there a current official hardcover?

Not confirmed. See the ISBN conflict noted above.
