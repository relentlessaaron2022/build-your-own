Reading Sink or Float? aloud

Read Sink or Float? as a game. On each object, stop and let the child say “sink” or “float,” then turn the page and find out, just as Zara and Amara do.

A simple way to read it

Do not rush the guesses. The book’s lesson only works if the child has time to be wrong, the way the characters are. After the story, if you try it at home, use unbreakable objects and stay with the child.
