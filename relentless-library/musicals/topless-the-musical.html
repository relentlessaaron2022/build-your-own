Topless The Musical: not released

Relentless named Topless as one of six musicals in this library. A catalog check for this page did not find the musical anywhere: no Apple Music album or song, no verified store URL. The status on these pages is Not released. That is different from the novel Topless, which is a published Relentless Aaron book and is not in this musicals section.

What was checked

An Apple Music / iTunes lookup for “Relentless Aaron Topless The Musical” returned no album and no song. No Relentless site page for a Topless musical was found at the obvious URLs (those paths returned 404). This page will not invent a listen button.

The novel is not a substitute listing

Relentless Aaron’s novel Topless is a published book from the mid-2000s, later also issued in a St. Martin’s Paperbacks edition, and it is not one of the four novels in this particular Relentless Library book batch. Trailers and other Topless video files exist on the box, but they were not treated as proof that a musical album is live. If Relentless later confirms a release, this page should be updated with a verified URL.
