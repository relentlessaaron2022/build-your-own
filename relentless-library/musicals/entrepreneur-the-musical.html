What is live, exactly

The Apple Music album Entrepreneur: The Musical is a complete 12-track release, not a single. A September 2025 LinkedIn post by DeWitt Gilmore also said the musical was live on all platforms. The verified listen link used here is Apple Music. The UnitedMasters artist page lists the title too; guessed album-slug URLs on UnitedMasters returned 404, so this page links the artist page and Apple Music, not a guessed UM album URL.

What the track titles suggest — without inventing a libretto

The song titles run from a leap of faith and unpaid bills through a finale and a child’s reflection. That sounds like a story about risk, money, family, and going big. This page will not invent scenes or lyrics that are not on the public track list.
