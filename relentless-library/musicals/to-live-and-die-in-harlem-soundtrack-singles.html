How they are labeled in the store

Apple Music files both under Relentless Aaron and uses the phrase “To Live & Die In Harlem Soundtrack.” That wording is “soundtrack,” not “original cast album.” This page keeps that distinction.

Artwork

Each single has its own official Apple Music artwork, saved in this page’s assets. That art is not the St. Martin’s novel cover and should not be used as if it were.
