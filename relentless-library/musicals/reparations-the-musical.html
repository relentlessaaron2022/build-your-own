The album and the site title

Apple Music lists the album as Reparations - The Musical. The site page at relentlessaaron.net/reparations-since-1776-the-musical/ (verified HTTP 200) uses Reparations — Since 1776: The Musical. This draft treats those as the same project with two public title forms, not as two different works, unless Relentless says otherwise.

What the work is about, in plain language

The site essay frames the musical as a score about a debt America has not paid, using 1776 as a reminder that independence was not freedom for everyone. It describes a mix of gospel, jazz, hip-hop, and call-and-response, and says the aim is recognition and conversation, not only applause. Track titles on the album — including Forty Acres, Juneteenth Gray, and We Still Build — sit in that same subject. This page stays at that public level and does not invent a scene-by-scene book.
