Coffee: The Musical, now live

Coffee: The Musical is live as a full 23-track album, dated September 5, 2025, on Apple Music. Relentless Aaron is the artist. It is a separate work from Entrepreneur: The Musical, and it is not the unrelated stage project at coffeethemusical.com.

A long musical album, with character songs in the titles

Twenty-three tracks is a full-length musical score, not a single. Titles name a barista, an accountant, a politician, a novelist, and others, plus a finale, “One Cup, One Song.” That is evidence of a story told in songs. This page does not invent a plot outline beyond what those titles already show.

Do not confuse it with another “Coffee The Musical”

A different project exists at coffeethemusical.com, associated with Robert Galinsky. That is not Relentless Aaron’s album. These pages do not link there as if it were his.

Questions

Is this the same as his Coffee Jazz albums?

No. Coffee Jazz is a separate series of instrumental albums listed on UnitedMasters. Coffee: The Musical is a 23-track musical.

Same release day as Entrepreneur?

Yes. Apple Music lists both albums on September 5, 2025.
