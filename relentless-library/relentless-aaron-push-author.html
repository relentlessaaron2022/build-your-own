Relentless Aaron, author of Push

Relentless Aaron is the author of Push. He published the first edition through his company, Relentless Content, Inc., in Mount Vernon, New York, and later placed a mass-market edition with St. Martin’s Paperbacks.

The byline and the company

Front matter in the early Relentless Content PDF names Relentless Aaron as author and Relentless Content, Inc., 9 West Prospect Avenue, Suite 314, Mt. Vernon, NY, as publisher, “published in arrangement with McRae Enterprises.” Text copyright is dated February 1, 2001; illustrations and concept are dated 2003. The character name Reginald “Push” Jackson is treated as a Relentless mark on that page.

How the author described the book at the time

The Library of Congress-style summary printed in that edition frames the book as crime, drama, and suspense fiction. It presents Push as a man who tries to balance rights and wrongs in Harlem’s underworld — not simply a coming-of-age portrait. Later bookstore copy puts more weight on the prison term and the dangerous return. Both descriptions come from publisher-facing text; this page paraphrases them and does not copy a jacket blurb word for word.

Questions

Did St. Martin’s originate the book?

No. The first paperback we can document is the Relentless Content edition (ISBN 0-9670542-1-4). St. Martin’s Paperbacks issued a later edition on February 6, 2007 (ISBN 9780312949693).

Is DeWitt Gilmore the same person?

Public biographical sources identify Relentless Aaron as the pen name of DeWitt Gilmore. The books themselves are credited to Relentless Aaron.
