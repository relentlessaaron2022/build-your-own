Push by Relentless Aaron: an urban fiction novel

Push follows Reginald “Push” Jackson through Harlem street life, prison, and a hard return home. Relentless Aaron first issued it through Relentless Content, then St. Martin’s Paperbacks published a mass-market edition in 2007.

What the story is about

This is a crime-and-suspense novel, not a how-to and not a memoir. The copyright page in the early Relentless Content files summarizes Push as Harlem’s “new judge and jury,” a man trying to settle scores in a city already crowded with hustlers and danger. Public listings describe a boy from Harlem who never meant to harm anyone, then loses his parents, is pulled into street fights and the drug trade, and becomes a powerful local figure while still trying to keep his sister and his baby nephew out of harm’s way.

The law catches up with him. Years later he comes home having, in his own mind, paid his dues — and finds new players and deadlier rules. That is the spine of the book as stated in catalogs and in Relentless’s own front matter. This page does not retell later chapters or invent scenes that are not in those sources.

Where this sits in the Relentless Library

Push is one of four novels in this Relentless Library track, alongside Sugar Daddy, To Live and Die in Harlem, and Extra Marital Affairs. To Live and Die in Harlem is the sequel; the musical of the same name, if it is produced, is tracked separately in the Musicals section and is not this novel.

There is also a later BLKCOMIXX comic project based on Push. These micro pages are for the novel.

Questions

Is this the same as Sapphire’s novel Push?

No. Relentless Aaron’s Push is a Harlem crime novel about Reginald “Push” Jackson. It is a different book from Sapphire’s 1996 novel Push.

Where can I buy it?

The 2007 St. Martin’s Paperbacks edition is listed at Amazon under ISBN 0312949693. Used copies of both editions also appear through bookstores. Confirm condition and seller before you buy.
