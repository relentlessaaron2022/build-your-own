Giving or collecting a copy of Sugar Daddy

If you are wrapping Sugar Daddy as a gift, shop the 2008 St. Martin’s paperback by ISBN 9780312949648 and be honest about the fact that it is adult street fiction, not a light romance.

A readable gift vs. a first-edition hunt

For a reader, the 2008 mass-market copy is the practical one. For a collector, the Relentless Content paperback is the scarcer object — and the one you should verify against the copyright page, because used listings sometimes mis-title mid-2000s street-lit paperbacks.

Who it is not for

This is not a book for the Relentless Library children’s shelf, and it is not a gentle relationship novel. Give it only to an adult who wants urban suspense.
