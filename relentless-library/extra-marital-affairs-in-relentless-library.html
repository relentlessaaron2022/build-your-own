The four-novel adult shelf

The novels in this draft are Push, Sugar Daddy, To Live and Die in Harlem, and Extra Marital Affairs. They share an author and a period. Only Push and To Live and Die in Harlem share a lead character.

What else is in the library — and what is not

Ricky Cool Book 1 and five Blacknificent Kids TV picture books are on the children’s side. Six musicals have their own section. SugarBert is off the list by Relentless’s instruction.
