Sugar Daddy in the Relentless Library

Relentless named this collection Relentless Library. Sugar Daddy is one of the four adult novels in the first library collection, not a children’s book and not a musical.

What this collection includes

The Relentless Library covers Push, Sugar Daddy, To Live and Die in Harlem, Extra Marital Affairs, Ricky Cool Book 1, five Blacknificent Kids TV picture books, and a Musicals section.

What this collection does not mix in

Do not confuse Sugar Daddy the novel with Relentless’s Coffee Jazz albums or with Coffee: The Musical. Those are music projects in the Musicals section or the wider catalog, not this book.

Questions

Is there a Sugar Daddy musical?

Not in the six musicals Relentless named for this page.
