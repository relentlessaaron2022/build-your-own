To Live and Die in Harlem, the sequel to Push

The early Relentless Content Push paperback already lists To Live & Die In Harlem as “Sequel To Push.” The later St. Martin’s edition of the sequel points back to Push on the back cover. That chain is documented. The musical is not part of it.

What “sequel” means here

The same lead returns, older and trying to work differently. Verified notes from the printed sequel show him two years into property work, with a company called Melrah, renovating a stretch of West 128th Street. The street-life past is not erased — the notes record a long prison term and a dangerous reputation — but the book’s opening movement is about building something legitimate, then being pulled back toward a wider plot.

What this page will not do

It will not recap Push’s entire plot, and it will not treat soundtrack singles as proof that the novel has been turned into a finished musical album. For music, see the Musicals section.

Is the screenplay on the box the novel?

No. Local files include a To Live and Die in Harlem document that is largely in screenplay form (INT./EXT., FADE OUT). The novel facts on these pages come from the printed St. Martin’s book and catalog records.

Is this a series beyond two books?

No further Push-novel sequel was confirmed for this draft.
