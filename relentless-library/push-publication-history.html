The publication history of Push

Push did not begin at a New York house. The first documented paperback came from Relentless Content. St. Martin’s Paperbacks issued a later mass-market edition on February 6, 2007.

From a Mount Vernon imprint to a national paperback

The early copyright page places the publisher at 9 West Prospect Avenue, Mount Vernon, and dates the text to 2001, with illustrations and concept dated 2003. Open Library’s Relentless Content record is December 19, 2003. That path — self-published street lit later picked up by St. Martin’s — matches how Relentless Aaron’s mid-2000s career is usually described in press of the period, including a 2006 New York Times profile of his deal.

What we could not find

A later sale-readiness check on the box (November 5, 2026) did not find a complete ~313-page re-release manuscript. The closest local file is a 69-page Relentless Content PDF covering chapters 1–7 only. These micro pages therefore lean on catalog records, the printed copyright page, and paraphrased public descriptions, not on a full later proof.

Questions

Was Push part of the St. Martin’s deal?

A 2006 New York Times piece described a St. Martin’s book deal. We have not seen the contract, so this page does not say which specific titles were in that deal. What we can say is that St. Martin’s Paperbacks did issue Push in February 2007.

Is a new edition coming?

Unknown. Nothing in the files used for this draft is a finished, sale-ready re-release.
