The novel vs. the musical: To Live and Die in Harlem

Relentless asked that the musical To Live and Die in Harlem be kept separate from the novel. This page is the divider. The book is a 2007 St. Martin’s paperback sequel to Push. The musical, as verified on Nov 15, 2026, has two live soundtrack singles and no full album found.

How to tell them apart

The novel has an ISBN (9780312949624), a St. Martin’s spine, and hundreds of pages. The musical project, so far as the catalog shows, is represented by two soundtrack singles: “Too Much Like Right” and “Keep Going Anyway,” both dated November 13, 2025. Those are audio listings, not a book.

Why the split matters on these pages

Search engines and shoppers will blur the title. Relentless Library therefore uses “the novel” in this cluster of pages and keeps musical status, links, and artwork in the Musicals section only.
