The lesson as a story

The book plan is short and clear. The girls test what floats, they are wrong at first, and they learn to try. That is a science habit told as a picture-book plot, not a worksheet.

Names in the files

Early brand notes marked most character names other than Malik as placeholders. These pages use the names in the book files: Zara, Amara, Dre, Jordan, and Kofi.

Questions

Does the book explain density?

The plan does not call for a textbook definition. It calls for a test in a bowl of water.

Can families try this at home?

The show’s episodes often end with a “try this at home” invitation. A bowl of water and safe household objects are enough — with an adult watching.
