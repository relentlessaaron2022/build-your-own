Relentless Aaron, author of Extra Marital Affairs

Extra Marital Affairs is one of the Relentless Aaron novels that went out under St. Martin’s Griffin, in the same mid-2000s window as his mass-market crime books, but in a trade-paperback trim rather than a rack-sized reprint of an earlier Relentless Content title — so far as this page could document.

Where it sits in his published list

A prior Relentless catalog brief places Extra Marital Affairs in 2006 at St. Martin’s Griffin, among other Griffin or Paperbacks titles of that period (Lady First, Single with Benefits, and the Paperbacks editions of Push and Sugar Daddy). A 2006 New York Times profile of Relentless Aaron’s book deal is part of the same era; this page does not claim, without the contract, that this exact title was inside that deal.

How the publisher framed him

Contemporary listings call him a force in street fiction and promise a story that stays unpredictable. Publishers Weekly ran a short notice under the hyphenated form Extra-Marital Affairs, still crediting Relentless Aaron and St. Martin’s Griffin, with a page count of 277 against the 288 in most retail records. That count is flagged, not silently “fixed.”
