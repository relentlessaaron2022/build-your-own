The record

Open Library, bookstore catalogs, and AALBC all list Extra Marital Affairs by Relentless Aaron, St. Martin’s Griffin, September 5, 2006, 288 pages, ISBN-13 9780312359355 / ISBN-10 0312359357. AALBC also places it under Macmillan as the publisher and files it in urban and street-lit categories. Original list price appears in some catalogs at $14.95 or, in later lists, $20.99 — treat current price as something to check, not as a fact on this page.

The page-count wrinkle

Publishers Weekly’s short notice used 277 pages. Most retail records use 288. This page records both. No second ISBN was confirmed.
