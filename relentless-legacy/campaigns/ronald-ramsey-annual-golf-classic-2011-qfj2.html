RONALD RAMSEY ANNUAL GOLF CLASSIC 2011

Ronald B. Ramsey Sr. (August 14, 1959 – September 25, 2025) represented Georgia’s 43rd Senate District from 2007 to 2015 and then served as a judge of the State Court of DeKalb County. Relentless Aaron produced campaign and event video for him.
