August 14, 1959 – September 25, 2025

In Memory of Judge Ronald B. Ramsey Sr. (1959–2025)

Ronald B. Ramsey Sr. represented Georgia’s 43rd Senate District, which included parts of DeKalb and Rockdale counties, from 2007 to 2015. In 2015 Governor Nathan Deal appointed him a judge of the State Court of DeKalb County, Traffic Division. He was earlier the first African American municipal court judge in Stone Mountain, and he is credited as a founder of the City of Stonecrest. He died September 25, 2025, at 66.

Relentless Aaron worked with Senator Ramsey for years: he produced the “Re-Elect State Senator Ron Ramsey 2010” campaign video and filmed the Ronald Ramsey Annual Golf Classic (2011), the senator’s HOPE Scholarship funding message, his 55th birthday celebration, and the August 2015 swearing-in of Judges Ronald Ramsey, Shondeana Morris, Kiesha Storey and Brian K. Ross to the State Court of DeKalb County. Those videos are linked below.

On Common Ground News reported that tributes poured in from DeKalb County leaders. The ABA remembered him as “a consensus builder” whose “glowing confidence, kindness and persuasiveness were legendary in bringing people together,” and noted that he wanted to be known first as a devoted husband, father, grandfather and church leader. He is survived by his wife, Doris, his son Ronald Jr. and his daughter Christyn.
