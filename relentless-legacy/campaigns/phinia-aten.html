Judge Phinia Aten — Chief Magistrate Judge, Rockdale County

Phinia Aten became Chief Magistrate Judge of Rockdale County, Georgia, after winning election in 2012. She was re-elected in 2016 and 2020, and in November 2024 she ran unopposed and received 37,519 votes. Before the bench she was a trial attorney for 14 years. She holds a law degree from Tulane and a public relations degree from Wayne State.

Relentless Aaron’s East Atlanta Multimedia filmed Judge Aten at a Rockdale domestic violence forum (video below). His August 2013 Val Archer show recap also lists “Honorable Judge Phenia Aten” among the officials who attended his First Saturday events at Starbucks. Campaign videos, flyers and photos from his work on her race were not found online. Those are on Relentless’s Facebook and in his files.
