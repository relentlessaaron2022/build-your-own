Relentless Legacy / SuperStar USA TV

Posted to YouTube December 20, 2007

Relentless Aaron Presents: The SuperStar USA TV Show-3

The SuperStar USA TV Show was a one-hour weekly cable variety show — music videos, live performances and interviews — that Relentless Aaron produced and directed out of Mount Vernon and New Rochelle, New York. His own descriptions say it ran in the New York tri-state area from 1989 into the mid-1990s.

From the video description

You never know what you'll witness when watching this show. We went everywhere, interviewed everybody, and had all-access to every event. All you other shows came AFTER! WORD!
