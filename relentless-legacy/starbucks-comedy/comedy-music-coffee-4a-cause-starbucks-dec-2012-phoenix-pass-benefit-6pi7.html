Posted to YouTube January 3, 2013

COMEDY, MUSIC & COFFEE 4a CAUSE @Starbucks Dec 2012 - Phoenix Pass Benefit

Part of “Comedy, Music & Coffee 4a Cause” (also billed as “Comedy, Karaoke & Coffee 4a Cause”), the free monthly First Saturday show Relentless Aaron produced at the Starbucks in Conyers, Georgia, to raise money for local charities.

From the video description

Relentless & East Atlanta Multimedia Presents: THANK YOU THANK YOU THANK YOU for supporting, sharing, and even mentioning our event all year. We've raised money for local charities, broadened awareness in our community, and ultimately we've birthed a brand new community initiative inside of the billion-dollar behemoth we all know as "Starbucks"

Join us as we film our biggest show to date, the funniest clean comedy. The best musical artists. All for the greatest cause.

During the past 2 years I had the chance to meet dentist Vanessa Dowdy and her 1yr old boy Mason. Mason needed a liver from birth. During the past 18months I learned the challenges of being on a donor list, trying to find a match, and the financial turmoil behind it all. Mason's story is so powerful, since he now has a liver and his yellow eyes have turned normal and white like yours and mine. Mason and family also join us…
