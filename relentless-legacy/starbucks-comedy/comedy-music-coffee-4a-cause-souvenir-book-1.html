Published on Issuu August 11, 2012

Comedy, Music & Coffee 4a Cause — Souvenir Book 1

From Relentless Aaron’s own publications about his years at the Starbucks in Conyers, Georgia.

The Idea: Aside from the day-to-day activities of E.A.M., which includes website development & maintenance, book writing & publishing, photography & video production, we’ve designed COMEDY, KARAOKE & COFFEE for a CAUSE in the longterm effort of giving back to our local charities here in East Atlanta. Our position is that our communities are suffering in one way or another; and we are challenged with “fixing” these concerns ourselves. Charitable initiatives & 501c Non-Profit Organizations have more or less identified these issues on various levels. However, entertainment has forever been an attraction that brings people together for whatever reason, whether planned, by design or random. So we figured, why not merge the two? Why not store faith in/support the charities, while also engaging with aspiring talent? And why not secure this project under the roof of a billion-dollar corporation…
