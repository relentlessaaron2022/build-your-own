COMEDY, MUSIC & COFFEE 4a CAUSE! - Produced by East Atlanta Multimedia, Inc

Part of “Comedy, Music & Coffee 4a Cause” (also billed as “Comedy, Karaoke & Coffee 4a Cause”), the free monthly First Saturday show Relentless Aaron produced at the Starbucks in Conyers, Georgia, to raise money for local charities.

From the video description

We're Continuing Something BIG at STARBUCKS! A free monthly event that helps local charities. The comedy is clean, funny, and the community connections are amazing. Come on out for a good cause. Every 1st Saturday, Starbucks, Conyers. 1515 Route 136, Conyers, Georgia 30035

Mission

To help raise money for local charities.

Description

We've been producing a series of shows that raise money for local charities. We're filming each show and developing a series of variety shows that will entertain and inform. The first of these shows will be aired/available here and elsewhere online.
