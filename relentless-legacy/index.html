Starbucks Jazz Nights

Wine Down Wednesdays & Live Jazz at Starbucks, Conyers, Georgia

ConyersJazz.com announced the series on April 1, 2014, one video promoted it as “Every Wednesday, Beginning May 18th 2014,” and the YouTube uploads run through December 2014. There is one page for each dated show, flyer or video found online. Shows with no public video or post aren’t listed yet. Those dates live in Relentless’s Facebook albums.

Starbucks Comedy Nights

Comedy, Music & Coffee 4a Cause — First Saturdays at Starbucks, Conyers

“Comedy, Music & Coffee 4a Cause” (it started as “Comedy, Karaoke & Coffee 4a Cause”) ran on the first Saturday of every month at 7 p.m. at Starbucks, 1515 Highway 138, Conyers. It was a free show that raised money for local charities such as Phoenix Pass. The 1st Anniversary show was in January 2012, and videos run through May 2014. There is one page for each show video, audio set or souvenir book found.

Concerts & Live Shows

From Run-DMC in Mount Vernon to live stages in New York and Atlanta

Relentless began promoting hip-hop concerts in Mount Vernon, New York, in his teens. Run-DMC, The Fat Boys, The Force MDs and Dr. Jekyll & Mr. Hyde all played his shows. Later he presented live sets in New York and Atlanta. There is one page per performance video found. Dates are given only where a source states them.

SuperStar USA TV

The cable TV show Relentless produced and directed

The SuperStar USA TV Show was a one-hour weekly cable variety show of music videos, live performances and interviews. Relentless produced and directed it out of Mount Vernon and New Rochelle, New York. His notes say it ran from 1989 to 1996 and reached over 2 million homes in the tri-state area.
