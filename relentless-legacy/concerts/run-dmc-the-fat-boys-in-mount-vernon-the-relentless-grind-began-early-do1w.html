Concert: 1984 (per Relentless) · video posted November 22, 2007

Run-DMC & The Fat Boys in Mount Vernon — “The Relentless Grind Began Early”

Relentless Aaron has said he began promoting concerts in his late teens in Mount Vernon, New York, booking acts including Run-DMC, The Fat Boys, The Force MDs and Dr. Jekyll & Mr. Hyde, and working alongside Russell Simmons and Tiny Wood. Relentless dates this Run-DMC concert to 1984. The video’s own title names Russell Simmons, Run-DMC and The Fat Boys in Mount Vernon, NY.
