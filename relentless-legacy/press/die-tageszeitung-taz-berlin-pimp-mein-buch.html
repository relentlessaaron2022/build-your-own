die tageszeitung (taz), Berlin: “Pimp mein Buch”

Germany’s taz profiled Relentless Aaron at a Harlem book launch for Extra Marital Affairs at the Hue-Man bookstore, calling him a leader of the African American literary scene on the verge of the mainstream.
