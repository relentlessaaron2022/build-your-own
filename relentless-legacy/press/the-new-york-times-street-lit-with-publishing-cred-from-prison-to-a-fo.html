February 14, 2006 · by Corey Kilgannon

The New York Times: “Street Lit With Publishing Cred: From Prison to a Four-Book Deal”

The Times followed DeWitt Gilmore — “a writer who goes by the pen name Relentless Aaron” — onto a prison-visit bus leaving Columbus Circle, where he sold his self-published novels, and reported on his six-figure, four-book deal with St. Martin’s Press. Relentless has described it as a front-page Arts story.
