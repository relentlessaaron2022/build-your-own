February 20, 2006

NPR News & Notes: “Roundtable: Black Authors in Spotlight”

Farai Chideya hosted a roundtable on Black literature with “author DeWitt Gilmore, who writes under the name Relentless,” Atria Books senior editor Malaika Adero and Odyssey Couleur editor Nick Chiles. Relentless joined from NPR’s New York bureau and discussed Push, The Last Kingpin and Platinum Dolls.

Sources: NPR
