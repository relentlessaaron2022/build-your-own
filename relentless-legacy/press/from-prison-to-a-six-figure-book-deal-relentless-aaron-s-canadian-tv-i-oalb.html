From Prison to a Six-Figure Book Deal | Relentless Aaron's Canadian TV Interview

Outlet / setting: Canadian television.

From the video description

Seven years in prison. A backpack full of books. A six-figure publishing deal. And a Canadian television crew that traveled all the way to New York City to capture the story.

This is the remarkable journey of Relentless Aaron, also known as DeWitt Gilmore, whose transformation from incarceration to mainstream publishing success attracted international media attention.

In this archival television feature, Canadian TV host Kim Clark Champniss introduces viewers to the man who helped redefine urban fiction and street literature.

The Canadian production traveled to New York City to follow Relentless Aaron on the streets where he built his publishing career, selling books directly to readers, one conversation at a time.

After serving seven years in prison, Aaron emerged with a collection of manuscripts and an extraordinary determination to succeed.
