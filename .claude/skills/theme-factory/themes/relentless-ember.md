# Relentless Ember

The house look of the Relentless brand and the BAMA app suite: warm ember on deep charcoal, built for book covers, reels, decks and landing pages.

## Color Palette

- **Ember**: `#f97316` - Primary accent, calls to action
- **Forge Red**: `#ff5a36` - Video and high-energy accent
- **Charcoal**: `#141416` - Dark backgrounds
- **Bone**: `#ecebe8` - Light text on dark, paper backgrounds
- **Ash**: `#75736e` - Secondary text and rules

## Typography

- **Headers**: Futura Bold (fallback: Avenir Next Bold, sans-serif)
- **Body Text**: Charter (fallback: Georgia, serif)

## Best Used For

Relentless book covers and launch pages, Cutroom title cards, course and pitch decks, YouTube thumbnails, app store screenshots.
