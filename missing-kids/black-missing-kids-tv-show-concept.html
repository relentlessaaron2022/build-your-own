How a show like this should behave, if it is built

It should be accurate. It should not dramatize a real child without the family’s and official partners’ say-so. It should put 911 and 1-800-THE-LOST on screen. It should send people to NCMEC and BAMFI, not to a comment section. These are standards for a future production, not a review of episodes that were not found.
