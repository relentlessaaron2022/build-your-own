Awareness and resources

Black missing children awareness

Awareness, here, means pointing people to official help — not circulating rumors, and not turning a real family into entertainment. Black missing children deserve the same urgency, care, and accurate information as any missing child.

Start with official organizations, not rumors

The Black and Missing Foundation (BAMFI) exists because families of missing persons of color have often had to fight for attention. NCMEC is the national clearinghouse for missing and exploited children in the United States. This draft does not invent statistics. Current figures, if you need them, should come from those organizations’ own pages, which change as cases change.

What not to do in the name of awareness

Do not share unverified photos, names, or “I heard” details. Do not guess a child’s fate. Do not use a real child’s image on a micro page unless you are quoting an official flyer and linking that official source on the same page. This first batch does none of that.

The show concept stays in its lane

If Black Missing Kids TV becomes a real series, it should still send viewers back to 911, local police, and 1-800-THE-LOST. A show is not a tip line.

Questions

Why doesn’t this page list cases?

Because Relentless’s brief said not to name or depict any specific missing child or case unless the information comes directly from an official source page linked here. This page is general awareness.

Can I help by sharing a post I saw?

Only if it comes from police, NCMEC, BAMFI, or another official source, and only if the information is still current. When in doubt, go to the official site.
