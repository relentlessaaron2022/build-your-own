NCMEC and 1-800-THE-LOST

1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678) is NCMEC’s hotline. If you have information about a missing or exploited child, that number is the one these draft pages repeat on purpose, along with 911 for emergencies.

What the hotline is for

If you have information, contact local police or NCMEC at 1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678). In an emergency, call 911. NCMEC also operates the CyberTipline for reports of online exploitation. Use the official forms and numbers, not a social-media inbox.

This draft is not NCMEC

Black Missing Kids TV, if it is built, should send people to NCMEC. It should not pretend to be NCMEC.

Questions

Is 1-800-THE-LOST only for missing children?

It is NCMEC’s hotline for missing and exploited children. Follow NCMEC’s own guidance on what to report there versus 911.

International cases?

Start with local authorities. NCMEC is a U.S.-based organization; see their site for how they work with other countries.
