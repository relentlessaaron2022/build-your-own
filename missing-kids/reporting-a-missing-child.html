Black Missing Kids TV / Reporting a missing child

Awareness and resources

Reporting a missing child

Reporting is official work. Call 911. Call 1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678). If you have a tip and are not sure who should receive it, give it to the police, to NCMEC, or through the FBI tip site. Do not substitute a message to a TV show or a comment on a video.

The numbers and forms this draft will actually link

  • 911 for emergencies and to reach local police
  • NCMEC: 1-800-THE-LOST (1-800-843-5678) and Get Help Now
  • CyberTipline: missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline or report.cybertip.org (HTTP 200)
  • FBI tips: tips.fbi.gov (HTTP 200)

NamUs

NamUs (the National Missing and Unidentified Persons System) also came back as a live official resource in this check (namus.nij.ojp.gov resolved to that site). It is an official system, not a social network. Use it as its own site instructs, in addition to — not instead of — calling police.

Shows and websites are not a report

If Black Missing Kids TV is later produced, a “contact the show” form still would not replace a police report. This draft will not add a fake tip inbox.

Questions

Can I report anonymously?

Follow the official tip site’s own rules. The FBI tip site and some police lines allow anonymous tips; this page will not promise what a local department will or will not do.

What if I am outside the United States?

Contact local authorities first. Then use official sites to see how they handle international reports.
