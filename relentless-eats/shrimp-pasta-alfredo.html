How we make our Shrimp Pasta Alfredo

Every order starts with shrimp seasoned and sautéed in a hot pan with herbs and spices until they turn pink, juicy and just slightly caramelized at the edges.

While the shrimp cook, the pasta boils to al dente so it keeps its bite on the ride to your door. The Alfredo sauce is stirred smooth and rich, then folded through the pasta so every strand is coated.

Want heat? Ask for Cajun spice and the kitchen works it right into the shrimp. The plate is finished with a warm garlic roll and a side of steamed broccoli for a fresh, green crunch.

The love that goes into every plate

Chef Relentless Aaron cooks this one like he is feeding family. No shortcuts on the sear, no rushing the sauce, and nothing leaves the kitchen until it looks like something he would be proud to put his name on.
