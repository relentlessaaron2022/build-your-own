How we make our Chicken Pasta Alfredo

The chicken is seasoned and grilled fresh until it is juicy inside with a light char outside, then sliced so every forkful gets a piece.

Pasta is cooked to al dente and tossed with steamed broccoli and a rich Alfredo sauce that coats without drowning. Cajun spice is available if you ask.

Each order comes with warm garlic bread and your choice of vegetable, so the plate feels like a full dinner, not a side dish.

The love that goes into every plate

This is the kind of food Chef Relentless Aaron grew up loving: generous, warm and honest. He keeps it simple and makes sure every plate is cooked with the same care whether it is the first order of the night or the hundredth.
