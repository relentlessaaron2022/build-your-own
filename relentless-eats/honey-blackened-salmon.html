How we make our Honey Blackened Salmon, Rice & Broccoli

The salmon is coated in a blackening seasoning, kissed with honey and cooked on high heat so the outside forms a dark, flavorful crust while the inside stays moist.

It is served over steamed rice with the vegetable of the day, often broccoli, steamed until bright green and tender-crisp.

Each order is two servings, packed so the fish and rice stay warm together.

The love that goes into every plate

Chef Relentless Aaron likes food that makes you feel good after you eat it. This plate is that idea in a box: honest portions, real flavor and nothing that weighs you down.
