How we make our Shrimp & Salmon Seafood Alfredo

Shrimp are seasoned and sautéed until pink and juicy, and the salmon is seared until golden with a flaky center.

The pasta is cooked al dente and coated in a smooth Alfredo sauce, then the seafood is folded in gently so the salmon stays in tender pieces.

It is finished, packed hot and handed off for delivery any time of day or night.

The love that goes into every plate

This is Chef Relentless Aaron's way of saying treat yourself. Three kinds of seafood, one sauce and a lot of care in how each piece is cooked.
