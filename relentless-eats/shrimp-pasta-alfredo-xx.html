How we make our XX Shrimp Pasta Alfredo (Double)

The XX starts the same way as the original: shrimp seasoned and seared in batches so every piece gets real color instead of steaming in a crowded pan.

A double portion of pasta is cooked al dente, then tossed with a generous pour of Alfredo sauce until it is glossy and clinging. The shrimp go on last so they stay tender.

Cajun spice is available on request. The order rounds out with garlic bread and steamed broccoli, packed so it travels well across Atlanta.

The love that goes into every plate

Doubling a recipe is easy. Doubling it without losing quality takes care. The XX is cooked to the same standard as a single plate, just more of it, because Chef Relentless Aaron believes a big order deserves the same attention as a small one.
