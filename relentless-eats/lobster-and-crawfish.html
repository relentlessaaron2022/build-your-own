How we make our Lobster Tails & Crawfish for Two

Lobster tails are cooked until the meat turns opaque and tender, and seasoned crawfish and shrimp are prepared alongside them.

Everything is plated over flavorful rice with steamed broccoli or the vegetable of the day. Cocktail sauce comes with the Lobster & Crawfish platter, and Cajun spice is available if you ask.

The Lobster Delight goes bigger, with two lobster tails, shrimp, rice, broccoli and whole seasoned Louisiana crawfish.

The love that goes into every plate

A lobster dinner should feel like an occasion, even when it shows up at midnight. Chef Relentless Aaron plates these like he is setting a table for someone he cares about.

What comes with it

Lobster tail, crawfish and shrimp with rice and a vegetable (Lobster & Crawfish for Two), or two lobster tails with shrimp, rice, broccoli and crawfish (Lobster Delight).
