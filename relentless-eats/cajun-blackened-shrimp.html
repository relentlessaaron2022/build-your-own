How we make our Cajun Blackened Shrimp

Shrimp are coated in a Cajun blackening blend and cooked fast on high heat so the seasoning forms a dark, flavorful crust.

They come off the heat the moment they turn tender so they stay juicy.

Blackening is all about the moment. Chef Relentless Aaron stays at the pan for every batch so the spice toasts without burning.
