How we make our Shrimp, Chicken & Combo Fried Rice

Rice is pan-seared in a hot wok-style pan so the grains toast and separate instead of turning mushy.

Peppers, onions, garlic and carrots go in for color and crunch, followed by shrimp, chicken or both.

It is all tossed with the chef's special seasoning and packed hot. The double sizes are built to feed two to three people.
