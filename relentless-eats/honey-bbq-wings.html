How we make our Honey BBQ Glazed Wings

Wings are seasoned and cooked until the skin is crisp and the meat pulls clean off the bone.

They are tossed while hot in a honey BBQ glaze so it caramelizes and clings to every wing.

Good wings are about timing and attention. Chef Relentless Aaron makes sure the glaze goes on at the right moment so they arrive sticky, not soggy.
