How we make our Jerk Wings & Rice

Wings are seasoned and cooked until crisp, then coated in jerk sauce or a curry BBQ sauce.

They are served over rice, and the curry BBQ plate adds vegetables, often broccoli, for a balanced box.

Everything is packed hot so the rice stays fluffy and the sauce stays glossy.

The love that goes into every plate
